# 🩹 CRS-173: Review and accept sourced condition occurrences

Status: open. Reviewed ingestion and correction remainder of CRS-085. The source/person-scoped `conditions` table exists in `src/server/migrations/007-condition-occurrences.sql` and uses the existing `record-versions.ts` journal. Accepted clinical-kind/review contracts do not expose it yet; that is this remainder. Owner priority, 2026-10-01: Conditions are active work, not deferred. Split further before implementation if this remains high effort, or obtain owner grading before merge. Personal status and user-facing lists are CRS-174; packet qualification is CRS-175.

## Owner decisions and purpose

Owner, 2026-09-30: **Conditions are a proper noun and a first-class entity**, perhaps working like prescriptions. **A Visit is a note with a visit tag, not a separate entity.** Not every visit needs recording; after-visit summaries, test results and prescriptions are the useful records. The app is not a date book. These decisions remain distinct from implementation recommendations below.

The chronic-condition tracker ([personas](../design/personas.md)) needs a named condition connecting longitudinal results, medications and notes. A receiving clinician needs a cited problem list; the low-effort self-tracker needs conditions discoverable when preparing for a checkup (CRS-126). Privacy-sensitive patients need control over which conditions reach a packet (CRS-175/150), including sensitive mental-health, reproductive and other records. Literal source assertions alone do not fulfill those jobs.

Jobs: [File and review](../design/personas.md#file-and-review), [Build a history](../design/personas.md#build-a-history), [Understand conditions](../design/personas.md#understand-conditions) and [Correct a record](../design/personas.md#correct-a-record). The caregiver needs the actual subject of each assertion; the operator needs complete occurrence accounting and recovery; the low-effort tracker needs a useful Conditions destination without extra identity questions for already reviewed evidence.

## Current evidence and limits

Checked against code during grooming, 2026-10-02. `src/shared/intake.ts:345–346` and `src/server/clinical-import.ts:289,523` accept observation, medication, procedure and document, excluding condition. Manual missing-record entry also excludes condition (`src/server/intake-manual-source-record.ts:176–181`), and the Import feed puts unsupported mappings under Documents (`src/app/features/import/ImportPage.tsx:118–132`). Extending the table alone will not complete this job.

The condition recovery tests insert rows directly with SQL (`src/server/test/condition-storage.test.ts:10–68`, `src/server/test/record-versions.test.ts:533–594`). Their problem-list, FHIR-shaped and visit-summary objects prove literal storage and recovery, not extraction, structured-format compatibility or reviewed acceptance. No provider-run qualification is claimed by this evidence.

Extracting a diagnosis named in a source and extrapolating a diagnosis from results, medications or symptoms are different operations. Current instructions forbid inventing diagnoses or causal relationships (`src/server/assistant-instructions.md:74–85`) and health interpretation during extraction (`src/server/clinical-instructions.ts:2`). This ticket extends sourced extraction; it does not adopt an inferred-diagnosis feature.

## Requirements

Use the [implemented condition table and journal](../data/data-model-contracts.md#condition-occurrence-storage); do not add a parallel store or acceptance path.
2. Keep each provider mention literal and immutable, one per source occurrence: problem-list entry, visit-summary diagnosis or FHIR Condition. Equal labels/codes never imply clinical equivalence; relating mentions is an explicit reviewed relationship (P5), not an automatic merge. Preserve the printed code and code system literally when present, without inventing a vocabulary normalization.
3. Current-schema retained clinical_object, condition and diagnosis assertions may be proposed for review without losing their literal source. Nothing is silently reinterpreted. Reuse original/source inspection and existing review choices; rejected/unsupported occurrences remain accounted for. The owner requires no old-schema migration before production; this requirement does not authorize one.
4. Include corrections/reclassification, related-record review and person reassignment in the existing clinical contracts, with stale-review rejection, exact contribution/original citations, patient/caregiver correction labels and complete history. Another person's records must stay out of Self's lists and exports. Exercise retries, loss of SQLite and recovery of accepted versions/originals.
5. Validate independently fictional problem-list, FHIR Condition, visit-summary and relative fixtures through review/acceptance. Stored provider active/resolved/verification text never establishes a personal current status. No condition is personally confirmed merely by import (CRS-174).
6. Update the intake contract, durable model instructions, data model and NOTE-EXPORTS documentation when behavior exists. Source assertions remain available while packets are qualified in CRS-175. Retire this ticket only after independent review; carry any unfinished subset into named remainder tickets.

## Grooming proposals and acceptance additions

These are implementation proposals, not additional owner decisions. Resolve the choices below before the implementation specification is finalized.

- **Eligibility and qualifiers.** Test an affirmative diagnosis, suspected/provisional diagnosis, rule-out/refuted diagnosis, negation, copied historical diagnosis, patient-reported history, educational instructions and family history. Preserve the assertion's literal wording, qualifier and actual subject. Decide which uncertain or negative assertions become condition occurrences and which stay supporting evidence; either route must keep them accounted for and prevent a confirmed/current presentation. An abnormal result or medication alone must not create a sourced diagnosis. Unreadable diagnosis text remains a located uncertainty, never a guessed label.
- **Report subject versus assertion subject.** A Self report containing “mother has X” must not create Self's X. A named relative's history can use existing People review; an unnamed relative remains literal evidence without an invented Person. A source's “family history of X” or risk/concern label must not be shortened into a claim that Self has X. Decide its supported representation without silently replacing the owner's family-history policy. Test mixed subjects within one report, including a report-wide Self confirmation followed by a relative assertion.
- **Structured formats.** Declare and test a precise supported subset rather than promise every FHIR export. Recommendation: use [HL7 FHIR R4 v4.0.1 Condition](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/condition.html) as the initial fixture baseline; preserve original fields and account for unsupported variants, extensions and unresolved references. Decide how subject and recorder/asserter references are reviewed without treating a recorder as the diagnosing clinician. R4 separates `clinicalStatus` from `verificationStatus`, and onset, abatement and `recordedDate`; preserve those distinctions and literal codes instead of flattening them into one status/date. This source informs representation, not certification or the personal-status policy. Other editions/formats require their own declared coverage and fictional fixtures.
- **Date policy.** Decide which source date, if any, orders an occurrence, and preserve separate onset, recorded, abatement and report issue facts with their printed precision. Unknown onset is not the report date. The table permits unknown/partial `effective_at` (`007-condition-occurrences.sql:9`; `record-versions.test.ts:590`), whereas [CRS-126's date rules](CRS-126.md#dates) require a full approved clinical date. Recommendation: retain literal unknown/partial condition dates without inventing an onset, and explicitly decide how this modifies or fits that rule. Source dates and the time of personal confirmation remain separate.
- **All review entry points.** Include Conditions filters/counts, supported field editors, bulk eligibility, original navigation, stale drafts, exact receipts, saved destinations and manual addition of a condition missed in retained source evidence. Reuse CRS-129's source-attestation gate when it lands; identity approval, source-text approval and clinical acceptance remain different assertions. Qualify caregiver selection, dismissal, correction/reclassification and person reassignment through the same path.
- **Repeated imports and amendments.** Distinguish exact operation replay, byte-identical deliveries, a new occurrence in a history re-export, changed assertions under a qualified issuing identity and unrelated equal labels. Test an old export after a corrected one in both upload orders. Reuse [CRS-143](CRS-143.md)'s decided current-version policy rather than invent another one; it does not select personal status. No new import or omission may silently reset or inherit a personal confirmation.
