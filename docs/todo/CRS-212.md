## User Story

As a [High-precision tracker](../design/personas.md#build-a-history),
I want to know whether the app can handle a long and varied medical history and what it leaves unfinished,
So that I can judge whether to rely on it for my own records.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. I look at the results of a complete example history.
2. I can see what was processed, what failed and what remains uncertain instead of mistaking partial progress for completion.
