# 📜 CRS-115: Complete durable text extraction for imported documents

Status: open; required production qualification for the already adopted CRS-126 P17 complete-text contract. Grooming classifies this as large effort and large impact: silently omitted readable evidence or loss after recovery prevents the Records assembler’s main job. This is not adoption of new formats or authority to run a paid provider.

## User Story

As a [Records assembler](../design/personas.md#read-unusual-evidence),
I want every readable part of my uploaded documents kept and easy to find,
So that I can check the evidence later without paying to read it again or losing details that produced no clinical record.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. Upload a document, scan or photo and let reading begin.
2. Check retained text against its page in the unchanged original, including notes and administrative pages.
3. Review located uncertainty and retry or correct unreadable sections without guessing.
4. Reload or recover the profile and find the same retained text without another AI reading.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** the forty-page/six-finding scenario in [CRS-126](CRS-126.md#complete-text-acceptance-scenario), **When** reading completes, **Then** all readable text is retained with original/member/page/region references, including the administrative-only phrase and legible annotation absent from the text layer; unreadable regions remain located exceptions.
- **Given** native, scanned, annotated, table and multicolumn fictional sources, **When** extraction is qualified against independent source expectations, **Then** wording, numbers, units, negation, order and table relationships are checked; omissions and uncertainty cannot be hidden behind a completed proposal or usable text layer.
- **Given** retained text and a correction, **When** the profile reloads or recovers, **Then** search and bounded assistant retrieval work without a new AI extraction, originals and correction history remain intact, and clinical acceptance is still separate.
- **Given** progress or completion counts, **When** they are displayed, **Then** pages read, text retained, unresolved regions and proposed/accepted clinical records are distinct. Retain-only exclusions and CRS-127's unread formats remain explicit; controlled fixtures do not qualify real-provider workflows.

Retain all readable source text, including administration, repeats, annotations and legible handwriting, with original/member/page/region references, reading order and table relationships. Locate uncertainty and unreadable material without guessing.

Keep text searchable and retrievable in bounded passages without another AI read after reload or recovery. Preserve originals, correction history and separate clinical acceptance. Reading progress, retained text coverage and clinical extraction are different measures.

The durable text layer exists; difficult handwriting/layouts, missed text-layer content, large sources beyond representation limits, human error discovery and complete provider workflows remain unqualified. Test native, scanned, annotated, multicolumn/table and administrative-only fictional documents. An agent can do the fictional-document work; complete real-provider workflows need the owner.

HEIC and multi-page TIFF uploads are not read today; that is [CRS-127](readme.md#crs-127).

## Where it stands

Background text capture now continues after a reviewable proposal and sends newly captured sections for reading. [Import processing](../import/processing.md#durable-evidence-and-review-boundaries) describes the measured page/span pins that keep earlier unaffected proposals reviewable. The remaining work is to qualify complete retained text on difficult layouts, scans, annotations, administrative-only material and provider workflows; the existence of a proposal is no longer a reason to stop capture.

## Current evidence and qualification limits

`src/server/test/intake-source-extraction.test.ts` exercises bounded child-text capture and preservation of human corrections. `intake-source-integration.test.ts` exercises retained-text search; `intake-source-text.test.ts` and `intake-source-text-vault.test.ts` exercise immutable revisions, publication interruptions and encrypted recovery. These establish useful mechanisms, not independently measured completeness on difficult handwriting or layouts. The mounted source-review journey in `src/tests/browser/source-text-review.test.ts` establishes inspection/correction and source navigation, not a calibrated missed-text detector or complete adopted-provider reading. Keep a separate expected-text oracle for the difficult-document qualification and report gaps by location rather than equating readable OCR with complete text.
