# CRS-116: Qualify and extend integrated import evidence review

Status: open.

The source review and host-authored draft paths exist. Remaining work includes:

- Independent omission detection and useful warning prioritization, with missed defects, false alarms and actual human effort measured separately.
- Acceptance races, and a durable “No clinical record needed” disposition for source material that yields no clinical record (see below).
- Qualified grouping of repeated material.
- Region-scoped re-reading with adequate context, trustworthy field geometry and bounded editing of large text revisions.
- Additional audit/repair diagnostics and human usability checks.

Moved out on 2026-09-30 (grooming): precise issue-to-field dependencies are now part of [CRS-118](CRS-118.md#issue-to-field-dependencies), and reconciliation of later model proposals with manually created records belongs with later-evidence reconciliation in [CRS-068](readme.md#crs-068), which absorbs CRS-070.

Do not treat a quiet issue list, reader confidence or manual text confirmation as a completeness certificate. Preserve original access, explicit clinical acceptance and accepted history.

## “No clinical record needed”

This disposition applies to source material, such as an administrative-only page, a cover sheet or a repeated notice, that a person has looked at and decided holds no clinical record. It records that decision durably, with the pages it covers, so the material stops appearing as unreviewed work while its text and original stay retained.

It is different from what exists today. `keep_original_only` (`LocalReviewDraft.disposition` in `src/app/features/intake/useReviewDrafts.ts`; `src/server/INTAKE.md` §Reviewed metadata and deferred work) is a terminal decision about one proposed record's candidate version: it keeps the original without accepting that record. It says nothing about source pages that produced no proposal. The scope described above is a grooming clarification (2026-09-30), not an owner decision; the owner confirms it.
