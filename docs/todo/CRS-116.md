# 🔍 CRS-116: Qualify and extend integrated import evidence review

Status: open.

## User Story

As a [Records assembler](../design/personas.md#file-and-review),
I want to check whether reading missed something and resolve source questions beside the original,
So that I can finish review with understood omissions and uncertainty rather than trusting a quiet warning list.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. Open an import's Needs attention sections and inspect the original beside its retained text.
2. Correct transcription, add a missing clinical record or review a proposed record as appropriate.
3. Where approved policy permits, record that inspected source material needs no clinical record.
4. Return later to the remaining work; text inspection and record acceptance stay separate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** fictional sources with independently known defects and negative controls, **When** omission warnings and repeated-material grouping are evaluated, **Then** record missed defects, false alarms and human effort separately; no confidence score or empty issue list certifies completeness.
- **Given** a source correction or concurrent acceptance, **When** a draft or clinical save is submitted, **Then** exact source/version and field dependency checks prevent stale acceptance, preserve unaffected records and keep accepted history and originals intact.
- **Given** the proposed source-level no-record decision is confirmed by the owner, **When** a person records it for inspected pages, **Then** its scope/disposition survives reload and recovery without discarding text or originals, accepting a clinical record or falsely covering changed evidence. Candidate-level keep-original-only is not that decision.
- **Given** region re-reading or large-text editing, **When** review proceeds, **Then** adequate surrounding context and evidence-backed geometry are retained and editing work is bounded; human usability qualification still uses the owner's chosen sessions and effort standard.

The source review and host-authored draft paths exist. [Import processing](../import/processing.md#source-attention-and-identity-follow-up) now documents the mounted Needs attention queue, section previews, explicit text inspection and manual missing-record entry. Its [attention counts](../import/processing.md#attention-queue-counts-and-completion) include distinct sections across visible originals and package members; `src/tests/browser/source-text-review.test.ts` exercises mixed selection, tab/loading guards and source review. This is existing review plumbing, not qualified omission detection or the proposed source-level no-record decision below. Unchanged-text inspection does not invalidate clinical dependencies; material transcription edits do. Preserve that distinction in the race qualification.

Remaining work includes:

- Independent omission detection and useful warning prioritization, with missed defects, false alarms and actual human effort measured separately.
- Acceptance races, and a durable “No clinical record needed” disposition for source material that yields no clinical record (see below).
- Qualified grouping of repeated material.
- Region-scoped re-reading with adequate context, trustworthy field geometry and bounded editing of large text revisions.
- Additional audit/repair diagnostics and human usability checks.

Moved out on 2026-09-30 (grooming): [precise issue-to-field dependencies](../import/processing.md#durable-evidence-and-review-boundaries) are implemented, and reconciliation of later model proposals with manually created records belongs with later-evidence reconciliation in [CRS-068](readme.md#crs-068), which absorbs CRS-070.

Do not treat a quiet issue list, reader confidence or manual text confirmation as a completeness certificate. Preserve original access, explicit clinical acceptance and accepted history.

## “No clinical record needed”

This disposition applies to source material, such as an administrative-only page, a cover sheet or a repeated notice, that a person has looked at and decided holds no clinical record. It records that decision durably, with the pages it covers, so the material stops appearing as unreviewed work while its text and original stay retained.

It is different from what exists today. `keep_original_only` (`LocalReviewDraft.disposition` in `src/app/features/intake/useReviewDrafts.ts`; `src/server/INTAKE.md` §Reviewed metadata and deferred work) is a terminal decision about one proposed record's candidate version: it keeps the original without accepting that record. It says nothing about source pages that produced no proposal. The scope described above is a grooming clarification (2026-09-30), not an owner decision; the owner confirms it.

## Owner decisions needed

- Confirm whether to adopt the proposed source-level “No clinical record needed” disposition and its page/change scope; the existing candidate-level decision does not settle that choice.
- The usability and review-effort checks: which fictional review sessions to run, and what counts as acceptable effort.
