## Decision and why

CRS-126 D6 keeps two facts apart: who a report was assigned to (immutable) and whether a printed name may match later reports (current, correctable). A remembered choice is current authority, so it must be visible and correctable like any other.

- **Low-effort self-tracker:** the "Always …" choice limits repeat prompts, which is why it exists.
- **Caregiver and high-precision tracker:** a rule chosen once must not silently outrank later corrections or another person's own name ("stale authority in either direction").
- **Receiving clinician:** a Jr./Sr. or twins collision is exactly where a standing rule can misfile a record ([CRS-130](CRS-130.md#medical-records-practice)).

## Where it stands

`ImportFutureNameChoice.tsx` offers "Always <person> / Always me / Ask each time" and writes a `Future name owner` entry. `assessIdentityPolicy` filters `exactOwners` to that owner before any other check, so after "Always me" a report printed with Robin's own full name goes silently to Self (the test "…explicit self future choice" asserts this). Nothing writes a new entry on a later ownership correction, and People does not show the rule. Reproduced by the grader: after "Always Robin Lane", a whole-report correction of a Robin Lane report back to Self offers no name decision, and the next report still goes to Robin. This contradicts `identity-review.md:126`, "Other people with the same spelling remain independent".

## Requirements

1. **Owner decision first:** may "Always X" outrank another saved person's primary name? Recommended: no. The choice records an active remembered name through the existing confirmation path, as CRS-134 specified, so a collision with another person's primary or manual name still asks (the G0 baseline).
2. Show each remembered owner in People beside the name it governs, with its date and a way to revoke it. Revoking returns the name to "ask".
3. A later ownership correction or explicit confirmation that contradicts the rule ends it or asks again. Test the sequence above.
4. **Hold versus a later whole-report correction.** Hold lookups are now keyed by `groupId`, and `exactReport` requires no hold. After a selected-record hold, a later whole-report correction leaves pending members at `confirmation_required` with the stale message "A selected record contradicted the earlier report assignment", although the dialog promised "Later records from the unchanged report use this person". Release the hold when a later whole-report correction covers the report, or say in the preview that the members stay held. Reproduced by the grader (sequence T6).
5. **Manual name kept.** When a manual Self name survives a `destination` or `superseded` decision, the preview says so and links to People to remove it (the CRS-134 recommendation, not implemented).
6. **A split's retained record** could say "Contribution from report B split to X on DATE" in details, PDF and evidence JSON. Decide whether this is wanted. Today it shows nothing, which is accurate. Conversely, a link target that always belonged to the destination person shows "Owner corrected / Previously attributed to …" because it received a corrected contribution; consider wording such as "Contribution from <person>'s report linked on DATE".
7. **Request count:** `OwnershipHistory` issues one `/record-history` request per displayed record (each Vision row, each Sources item). Batch it, or fetch on demand, so requests do not grow with the list.
8. **Tests missing:** G3 acceptance as a repository test (the grader's probe passed); G2 with a distinct later report; receipt reuse where nothing was challenged; rebuild equivalence for challenged names, holds and remembered owners; the Self-name veto and `canonicalSelfMatch` with a challenged name; the browser ownership journey, run and recorded.
9. **Docs:** rewrite the two sentences CRS-134 flagged as overstated (`identity-review.md:74-75`, `:125`), and state why unresolved is not removal.

## Owner decisions needed

Beyond requirement 1, CRS-134's five open questions were settled along its recommendations without a recorded decision. Confirm or change each:

- A report's own unaffected receipt keeps authorizing its own later members but is not reused for other reports.
- A `destination` or `superseded` correction never silently removes a manual Self name.
- A challenged name neither vetoes a new Person nor counts as a Self match.
- Undo is a "Review undo" that opens a reviewed reverse preview, not a one-click reversal.
- The "(you?)" pill becomes "Corrected to X" after a whole-report correction.
- Packets: earlier packet inclusion is disclosed as not recorded. Should a durable packet-membership receipt be added so the app can say who may need a corrected copy?
