# 🧺 CRS-216: Build the shared selective-packet foundation

Status: open. Split from CRS-150 during PM review, 2026-10-02, to remove a dependency cycle: Conditions packet qualification needed exclusions, while the whole packet ticket waited for Conditions. This foundation can be designed independently of Conditions; CRS-175 then qualifies Conditions against it, and CRS-150 composes the annual/specialist/dentist/emergency presets. Use independent review for coherent implementation; separate contract, implementation or qualification only where those complexity boundaries help. No feature has shipped in this split.

Serves the Privacy-sensitive patient, Caregiver and Receiving clinician's [share a packet](../design/personas.md#share-a-packet) job. Existing person-scoped packets, immutable previews and original companions are described in [note exports](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md). Category and individual-record exclusions, persistent withholding preferences and packet-specific omission disclosure do not exist yet. The new companion checks identify missing acceptance coverage, not a demonstrated leak.

## User story

As a [Privacy-sensitive patient](../design/personas.md#share-a-packet), I want to withhold selected records from every part of a shared packet while keeping my own history intact, so sensitive information does not leak through a PDF, JSON, narrative or original companion. Disclosure choices need one frozen selection across formats; removing a visible row alone is insufficient.

## Requirements transferred from CRS-150

1. Implement manual category and individual-record inclusion/exclusion over one selected person's records. Categories use knowable metadata: accepted kind, an explicit date window and person-applied tags; never model-inferred sensitivity. Preserve the single-subject guard and the patient's complete in-app history.
2. A persistent per-record “always leave out of packets” preference overrides later presets and remains changeable by the profile user. Store only changed preference data and stable references; do not copy whole sources, histories or an ever-growing membership snapshot on every write. Preserve preferences through supported-format recovery/export without changing clinical evidence.
3. If anything is withheld at the person's request, share the generic notice “Some records were left out at the patient's request” or equivalently clear text. PDF and shared evidence must not reveal excluded labels or counts. The private preview shows actual choices, counts and titles and warns about empty categories. Distinguish user withholding from unresolved ownership or unread sources; do not label extraction failures as a patient choice.
4. Include selection/preferences and actor/approval metadata in the immutable preview fingerprint; stale choices cannot silently print. Preview, PDF and evidence JSON must agree on subject, selected content, citation scope and disclosure. A compact recipe may reference stable IDs/predicates, but its frozen output membership must be deterministic.
5. Qualify exclusions across normalized records, included note text, raw assertions, evidence JSON, attachments, shared-source context and original companions. A selected result can share a source with an excluded condition. Settle the shared-original policy before implementation: recommendation is withhold the conflicting companion by default and require an explicit disclosed choice before including it. A separately labelled derivative is an alternative scope decision, not permission to edit the immutable original. Never claim a condition is excluded while silently offering a companion that discloses it. Define how conflicts are detected and how unchecked narrative/original content is described; do not claim universal semantic redaction from a record-ID filter.
6. Use independently fictional Self/managed-person fixtures with sensitive content repeated in a source, note, attachment and structured evidence; shared sources; unresolved ownership; empty categories; and an exclusion changed after preview. Inspect actual PDF text and evidence JSON, companion listings/content and reopened stale previews. Preserve originals/accepted history and verify preference replay/cache-loss. No private records or live model calls are needed for the deterministic boundary.
7. Provide a kind-extensible selection contract that CRS-175 can use once reviewed Conditions and personal status exist. Do not require Conditions ingestion to deliver selection for currently supported kinds. Condition-specific status/provenance/qualifier qualification belongs to CRS-175; job presets and one-page layout belong to CRS-150; release print inspection remains CRS-073.

## Decisions and completion

Resolve the shared-original disclosure policy here, with CRS-175 using the same choice instead of a second exclusion system. A recipient/copy tracking feature is not part of this foundation.

Update maintained packet procedures only when implemented, run focused selection/PDF/recovery tests plus formatting/types and full PR CI, obtain an independent passing grade, and delete this ticket with any unfinished subset explicitly carried forward.
