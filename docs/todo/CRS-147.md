# CRS-147: Make printed packets safe to hand to someone else, for any person in the profile

Status: open. Filed during backlog grooming on 2026-09-30 from a read-only review of printed output against recent import, ownership and partial-save work. [CRS-073](readme.md#crs-073) only inspects today's output on the release build; this ticket changes what is printed. Line numbers were reported at `1e6fa50`; re-verify before editing.

## Decision and why

Profiles are never shared, and the people a caregiver manages do not log in (owner, 2026-09-30). When a caregiver wants someone else — a family member, a new provider — to see a person's records, they print that person's PDF output. So printed output is the sharing mechanism, and it must be as correct for a managed person as for Self.

- **Caregiver:** needs a complete, correctly labeled packet for the person they manage, started from that person, listing themselves as the caregiver.
- **Receiving clinician:** needs to tell a provider's value from one the patient or caregiver corrected, and must never receive another person's record.
- **Privacy-sensitive patient:** needs to leave sensitive records out of one packet without the packet pretending to be complete.
- **Low-effort self-tracker:** needs the right packet for the visit without choosing records one by one.
- **Emergency contact:** needs a plain one-page summary, not a clinical packet; this is the low-tech alternative to [CRS-145](CRS-145.md).

## Packets are chosen by the job, not built from everything

Owner, 2026-09-30: most visits don't need everything. Exclusion works per category, with finer choices where a job needs them. Each job below is a starting preset: the person can add or remove categories and single records, and a packet with anything left out says "Some records were left out at the patient's request" without saying which.

| Job                                                         | What the packet needs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Notes                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Annual visit with a new provider** (the most useful case) | Family history, conditions, past surgeries and procedures, current medications, allergies, immunizations, and recent tests that belong in an annual (for example, labs from the last few months). Moxie drafts an annual note from these, and the person reviews it before it prints. | These are the questions a new provider always asks. It depends on conditions becoming records ([CRS-085](CRS-085.md)). Where family history is stored today has not been checked. |
| **Specialist with a narrow need**, such as an eye doctor    | A single note of relevant conditions and medications, plus the relevant records (for example, eyeglass prescriptions)                                                                                                                                                                 | Choosing which conditions are "relevant" is a proposal the person confirms, never an automatic filter.                                                                            |
| **Dentist**                                                 | Current medications, allergies, and conditions that matter for dental care                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Earlier dental X-rays are mostly for the person's own records, kept as companion originals.                                                                                       |
| **Emergency one-pager**                                     | Requirement 8                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | For a wallet or the fridge, and for the emergency contact.                                                                                                                        |
| **Move or checkpoint everything**                           | Everything, in portable form                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | This is not a printed packet. See [CRS-148](CRS-148.md).                                                                                                                          |

**Categories.** Categories must be ones the app knows for certain: record kind, time window, and tags the person applied. The app cannot reliably tell whether a record is sensitive (mental health, reproductive, HIV), and a wrong guess leaks the record the person meant to hide. So a sensitive category is one the person marks, never one the app infers.

## Where it stands

- Visit briefs and provider packets render as server HTML, and Playwright Chromium turns them into PDF (`exportPdf` in `src/server/note-exports.ts`). Provider packets carry `provider-evidence.json`; companion originals are separate downloads ([note exports](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md)).
- **Starting point.** A whole-record packet starts from the Self profile only (`canPrint` in `NoteEditor.tsx`; `src/app/features/notes/README.md`). For a managed person the caregiver must create a note that person owns and export from it; the subject then comes from the note's owner (`exportPerson`), and tests keep that single-subject scope (`note-ownership.test.ts`).
- **Managed-person packets are thinner and mislabeled.** Unmatched allergy, condition, visit and immunization assertions are added only when `personId === 'patient'` (the "no normalized table yet" block in `packetSelection`). The PDF still says such assertions may appear, and the dialog promises care contacts, which are dropped for anyone but Self.
- **Corrections.** Ownership corrections print ("Owner corrected on DATE"), and since `1e6fa50` only on the record that received a contribution. Direct field corrections and reclassifications (`record-corrections.ts`) are not printed at all: a corrected value prints as an unmarked "Provider/source assertion". The kind of ownership correction (moved, split, linked) is not printed, so a link target that always belonged to its person reads "Previously attributed to …" (see also [CRS-139](CRS-139.md) requirement 6).
- **Disclosures.** Partly saved reports and unread sections or located exceptions are printed in provider packets only; visit briefs have `readingGaps: []`.
- **No exclusion.** Provider mode's only choice is which extra notes to add.
- **Self fallback scope (suspected, not reproduced).** The Self block adds unmatched clinical `source_records`, which have no person column, filtered by provider but not by person. A record already linked to anyone through `evidence` is excluded, which is how person-owned sources are recorded (`person-source-evidence.ts`), so a leak needs a relative's clinical source record with no evidence row. Whether any import path produces one is untested.
- **Tests.** Server HTML assertions and eight mounted dialog tests exist. No automated test renders a real brief or provider PDF, and none renders a managed person's packet; `src/scripts/verify-note-export.ts` is manual and uses the legacy detailed mode.

## Requirements

1. Start a provider packet from any person's profile, not only Self, keeping the single-subject guard.
2. Give managed people the same content as Self, including their own allergy, condition, visit and immunization assertions. Scope those by person rather than by `personId === 'patient'`, and add a test that a relative's unmatched assertion never reaches Self's packet (write it first; it settles the suspected fallback leak).
3. List the caregiver and the person's own emergency contacts on a managed person's packet, and make the dialog and PDF copy match what is actually included.
4. Print direct field corrections and reclassifications beside the value, with date and reason, and say who made them. On a managed person's packet: "Corrected by Sam Ortiz (caregiver) on DATE — not from a provider." On Self's own packet: "Corrected by the patient on DATE" (owner, 2026-09-30). Carry the same in the evidence JSON.
5. Print what kind of ownership correction happened (moved, split from a shared record, linked to an existing record).
6. Include the partial-report and unread-section disclosures in visit briefs whenever a cited source qualifies.
7. Packet presets by job, as in the table above. Categories come from record kind, time window and person-applied tags. Add a per-record "always leave out of packets" flag, and the withheld-records disclosure. Moxie's annual-note draft is reviewed by the person before it prints.
8. After requirements 1–4 (owner, 2026-09-30), offer an optional one-page emergency summary: identity, confirmed current medications, recorded allergies and conditions with citations, contacts, and who prepared it, in plain language without clinical interpretation.
9. Say who confirmed a medication's current use (the person, or the caregiver on their behalf). Leave room for page-approval state from [CRS-129](CRS-129.md).
10. Add automated real-PDF tests (render, then extract text) for brief, provider and managed-person packets; then CRS-073's manual release-build pass inspects the result.
