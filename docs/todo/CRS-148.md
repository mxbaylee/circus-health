# 🧳 CRS-148: Export everything in a portable form

Status: open. Filed during backlog grooming on 2026-09-30, from the "move or checkpoint everything" job now contrasted with [selective printed packets](CRS-150.md).

## User Story

As a [High-precision tracker](../design/personas.md#build-a-history),
I want to take all my records and their history to storage or tools I choose,
So that I can move or keep a checkpoint without depending on this app.

## Plain-English walkthrough

1. Choose one person or my whole profile to export.
2. Review what will be included, any omissions and the agreed protection choice.
3. Save the export where I choose and check that its contents match the supplied inventory.

## Given–When–Then acceptance

The detailed contracts, owner decisions and evidence gates below remain required.

- **Given** a fictional profile with originals, accepted versions, notes, people, relationships and corrections, **when** the chosen scope is exported, **then** the manifest covers every included file and hash, originals match byte-for-byte and documented records preserve version and ownership history under requirements 1–2.

- **Given** a large export and the adopted protection policy, **when** the user saves it, **then** output streams rather than loading the whole profile into memory, exposes the agreed plaintext/encryption handling and puts no records in repository files or logs.

- **Given** the export and its manifest, **when** the fictional round-trip check runs, **then** all files and declared contents are verified; app re-import and standard formats are required only as resolved by the owner decisions, and an encrypted archive copy is not called a portable export.

## Decision and why

The owner wants a Google Takeout–style export containing everything (owner, 2026-09-30). It serves two uses: moving to another storage provider, and keeping a checkpoint somewhere else. Unlike the [selective printed packets](CRS-150.md), this output is not written for a reader. It must be complete, and another tool must be able to read it without this app.

- **High-precision tracker:** has years of records from about fifteen sources. The export is the way out, and the insurance against losing them.
- **Operator:** a checkpoint outside the laptop, usable even if the app itself is gone.
- **Caregiver:** moving a managed person's records to that person or to another caregiver.

## Where it stands

- Backups today are user-managed copies of the encrypted durable directory plus the recovery kit ([profile storage](../data/profile-storage-and-rebuild.md)). They are application-specific encrypted authority, not a documented app-independent portable export.
- The legacy recovery CLI is retired and its npm aliases are removed; direct calls refuse before touching an archive/destination. The supported [encrypted-directory copy](../setup/deployment.md#backup-and-recovery) is not a portable Takeout export.
- Project-owned portable formats use the `circus-health` slug, and `health-record-v1` is an existing neutral contract ([profile management](../features/profile-management.md)).
- No in-app action exports every original, record, note, correction and version in a portable form. The current `vault-app.ts` rejects the retired backup action; note exports in `src/server/note-exports.ts` produce reader packets, and the maintained independent restore guide still lists general portable export as open. These checked surfaces do not constitute an exhaustive export implementation audit.

## Requirements

1. One action exports a person's records, or the whole profile. The export contains:
   - every original, byte-for-byte;
   - accepted records in a documented, standard-leaning format (for example, a FHIR bundle alongside `health-record-v1`);
   - notes, people and their relationships;
   - corrections and ownership history;
   - version history;
   - a manifest listing every file with its hash.
2. The export states what it omits, if anything: for example, working caches, or model state that doesn't belong in it.
3. The export's contents are health records. Nothing lands in the repository or logs, and where it goes is always the person's choice. Decide whether the export is encrypted (for example, with a passphrase) or plaintext with a clear warning.
4. Its size scales with the data, and it streams to disk rather than holding the profile in memory (see [CRS-043](CRS-043.md) for related intake streaming work). This export must implement its own bounded output path; it need not wait for upload-cap removal or ZIP ingestion. Reuse shared streaming helpers where suitable without making the unrelated intake feature a prerequisite.
5. Add a round-trip test on fictional data: export, then check against the manifest, and ideally re-import into a fresh profile with nothing lost.

## Owner decisions needed

- Plaintext with a warning, encrypted with a passphrase, or a choice between the two?
- Should re-importing an export into this app be a requirement, or only readability by other tools?
- Which standard formats matter most (FHIR, C-CDA, CSV for measurements)?

The one-person export also needs an explicit shared-original boundary: an original can contain another managed person's report or assertion. Decide whether this action carries that immutable original with a visible disclosure, omits it with an honest incompleteness statement, or offers a separately labeled derivative. The whole-profile “everything” requirement remains intact. Reuse the [implemented packet companion/disclosure design](../features/selective-packets.md) where suitable, but an app-independent full export must not silently inherit packet exclusions and call itself complete. Neither packet presets nor Conditions ingestion is a prerequisite for exporting currently supported authority; extend the inventory when those contracts land.

## Completion
