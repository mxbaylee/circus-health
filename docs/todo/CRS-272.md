# 🪜 CRS-272: Finish recovery of interrupted setup copies

Status: open; unpublished-copy recovery remainder of CRS-245. Its
[admission repair](../security/profile-encryption.md#unlock-and-setup-admission)
allows valid queued unlocks and setup copies to finish without elapsed expiry
interrupting them. It does not resume an unpublished partial copy across a process
interruption. The owner has approved restarting unpublished work while preserving
exact committed-head recovery; implementation qualification and documentation remain.

## User Story

As an [Operator](../design/personas.md#operate-and-recover),
I want to recover a profile copy after my laptop or the app stops,
So that I understand what work is retained and can finish safely without losing
accepted records or repeating avoidable work.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. I start copying an existing profile into a new one, but the app stops before
   the copy finishes.
2. I reopen the app and approve recovery of the setup.
3. The app explains whether it can continue the saved work or must copy again,
   following the approved restart-or-committed-resume policy below.
4. If the new profile already has a committed copy, recovery uses that copy even
   if the original profile has since changed or been locked.

## Owner Decision

Owner, 2026-10-16: an actually interrupted **unpublished** copy may restart.
After the first target manifest commits, recovery must preserve exact
committed-head resume. A new authenticated partial-stage checkpoint/reuse
protocol is not required by this policy.

The original requirement was that resumed setup reuse completed copy work, or
that its open question record why it cannot. Admission-only expiry removes one
cause of discarded work; the accepted restart policy now settles behavior after
a real interruption. Explain the repeated work honestly to the Operator, while
preserving the High-precision tracker's [existing history](../design/personas.md#build-a-history).

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Recovery follows an explicit policy

Given a fictional copy interrupted before its first target manifest commits,
When the operator recovers setup,
Then recovery restarts unpublished work with an explicit explanation that the
unfinished copy is not a verified committed recovery point,
And maintained documentation and focused recovery tests describe that behavior,
And evidence counts copied/reused bytes, rereads, hashes and durable writes rather
than imposing a time bound on model or copy work.

### Scenario: Restarted work has fresh authorization

Given a retained unpublished stage after a process interruption,
When the operator restarts the copy,
Then the new attempt establishes fresh setup admission, source-head and membership
bindings with fresh physical witnesses rather than trusting partial-stage contents,
And changed, corrupt, incomplete or unrelated work cannot become target authority,
And cancellation, source or target revocation and replacement setup still refuse
at checkpoints and the final seal without unauthorized activation,
And a process restart never resurrects an old in-memory admission lease.

### Scenario: Committed target recovery remains exact

Given an authorized first target head committed before a later refusal,
When the operator retries using the same still-valid setup session or a newly
validated recovery session,
Then recovery uses that exact target head without requiring its former source,
And later source edits are excluded and source locking does not prevent recovery,
And late refusal retains committed history without opening the profile or
granting a session; it may retain a registered, locked profile.

### Scenario: Recovery and orphan cleanup agree on stage ownership

Given retained partial stages and unrelated profile files,
When recovery or [CRS-249](CRS-249.md) cleanup examines them,
Then a live attempt or committed target is not swept as abandoned,
And genuinely orphaned stages can be reclaimed under an explicit ownership rule,
And the vault, keyring and unrelated files remain protected.

## Current Implementation and Scope

`src/server/encrypted-profiles.ts` owns setup admission, copy staging, publication
and recovery. `src/server/test/encrypted-setup-cooperation.test.ts` and the
`profile-admission` tests cover admitted work, revocation and committed-head
recovery, including source changes and locking. Cooperative unpublished copy
attempts still create a fresh `.setup-stage-*`; in-process cleanup does not establish
cross-process stage reuse or crash-orphan reclamation.

The admission repair preserves original source and target proofs throughout the
publishing attempt, then removes the source dependency only for future recovery
of the committed target. This ticket must not reopen that correctness boundary
or treat committed accepted history as disposable staging.

CRS-249 separately owns orphan-stage cleanup, refused ownership snapshot catalogs
and applicable contributor package spools. Coordinate only the setup-stage
ownership boundary; those other cleanup requirements do not belong here.

The production gate remains until this approved policy is qualified. Medium
effort covers the coordinated recovery, interruption tests, work accounting and
documentation checks; the previously possible partial-stage protocol is out of
scope. Medium impact reflects repeated work after an actual interruption now
that elapsed expiry no longer prevents completion. No owner choice blocks this work.

## Queue context

Qualify recovery of interrupted unpublished setup copies by restarting them with fresh authorization and an explicit explanation; preserve exact committed-head resume. Owner approved this policy on 2026-10-16; authenticated partial-stage reuse is not required.
