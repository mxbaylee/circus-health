# 🪪 CRS-144: Migrate the clinical, candidate and report-group identity keys together

Status: open. Opened during backlog grooming on 2026-09-30 to replace CRS-046 ("Finish subject/member-safe clinical identity and persisted-key migration qualification; never merge records across people"), with CRS-042 ("Explain report splits and preserve report/person/date/header relationships without merging equal-looking clinical events") merged in. [Import reconciliation](../import/import-reconciliation.md#persisted-identity-domains) already said the two keys need one joint migration. Line numbers were checked at `1e6fa50`; re-verify before editing.

## Decision and why

Design and run one versioned migration of all three persisted identity keys, with an explicit equivalence contract. Fixing one hash on its own is unsafe: making the report key coarser can merge people's reports, and making the clinical key finer can split one person's history. Never merge records across people.

- **Caregiver:** a family ZIP with a parent's and a child's reports that share an issuing identifier must never collapse into one record. Today the guard refuses rather than merges, which is safe but leaves those reports pending.
- **High-precision tracker:** a running-header report split across pages turns one report into several review groups, so the same identity question is asked more than once and one report's records look unrelated.
- **Receiving clinician:** a record merged across people, or one report shown as several, both misstate provenance.

## Where it stands

- **Clinical key (v1) ✅.** `clinicalSourceIdentityV1` (`src/server/intake-source-identity.ts:13-23`) hashes the issuing system and record ID, or else the original hash, locator and envelope ID. It leaves out report subject and package member. `INTAKE_SOURCE_IDENTITY_VERSION = 1` (`:11`), and the comment asks to keep the bytes stable until a versioned migration.
- **Candidate key (v1) ✅.** `candidateSourceIdentityV1` (`:25-40`) also binds the delivery (`file.id`) and, when present, the report member and full subject anchor, with different whitespace handling.
- **Report-group key ✅.** `src/server/intake-report-groups.ts` ~:70-83 hashes the original ID and hash, source system, member, the full report and subject anchors including their locators, and the proposed subject role. The locators are what split a running-header report across pages.
- **Refuse, never merge ✅.** Unsafe reuse of a clinical key across subjects or members returns `CLINICAL_SOURCE_SCOPE_COLLISION` (`src/server/clinical-source-scope.ts:17`; thrown at `src/server/clinical-import.ts:1458`) before any accepted write ([refuse unsafe clinical source reuse](../import/import-reconciliation.md#refuse-unsafe-clinical-source-reuse)). As a result, repeated reports in different ZIP members stay pending even when bytes and subject match, and cross-member consolidation is off until this migration lands.
- **No explanation of a split** is shown in the review UI (grooming report, 2026-09-30; not re-checked here).
- The consumers a migration must map are listed in `import-reconciliation.md` ("Persisted identity domains"): candidate and group versions, drafts, identity and source confirmations, acceptance and contribution receipts, clinical entity IDs and evidence links, and individual correction exceptions.

## Requirements

1. Write the equivalence contract for v2 keys: what makes two occurrences the same clinical source, the same candidate and the same report, including whitespace, originals, member and subject scope. Equal values, dates or names never establish identity (P5).
2. Report grouping no longer splits a coherent running-header report across pages. Any split that remains is explained in review, naming what differs (member, subject, source system).
3. Migrate every persisted consumer listed above in one versioned step. Old archives rebuild without losing accepted history, and no earlier human decision (identity confirmation, correction exception, relationship decision) is widened to cover more than it did.
4. Restore cross-member acceptance and consolidation only after (1)–(3), and only where the contract says two occurrences are the same source in the same subject scope.
5. Writes scale with what changed (`AGENTS.md`, [record versions](../data/record-version-storage.md#source-text-write-amplification)): the migration appends mapping records; it does not rewrite whole journals.
6. Fictional-fixture tests: a running-header report across three pages forms one group; a family ZIP whose two members repeat an issuing ID for different people stays separate; the same report in two ZIP members for one person consolidates after migration; an archive written with v1 keys rebuilds identically before and after.

## Owner decisions needed

- Whether the migration runs automatically on upgrade or behind an explicit, reversible step for existing archives.
