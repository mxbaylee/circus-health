# 🪪 CRS-144: Define subject-safe clinical, candidate and report-group identity

Status: open. Opened during backlog grooming on 2026-09-30 to replace CRS-046 ("Finish subject/member-safe clinical identity and persisted-key migration qualification; never merge records across people"), with CRS-042 ("Explain report splits and preserve report/person/date/header relationships without merging equal-looking clinical events") merged in. [Import reconciliation](../import/import-reconciliation.md#persisted-identity-domains) identifies the related persisted identity domains. Line numbers were checked at `1e6fa50`; re-verify before editing.

## Decision and why

Define one coherent current-format equivalence contract for all three persisted identity domains before changing any key. Old-schema archive migration is outside the owner's pre-production scope (2026-10-01). Fixing one hash on its own is unsafe: making the report key coarser can merge people's reports, and making the clinical key finer can split one person's history. Never merge records across people.

- **Caregiver:** a family ZIP with a parent's and a child's reports that share an issuing identifier must never collapse into one record. Today the guard refuses rather than merges, which is safe but leaves those reports pending.
- **High-precision tracker:** a running-header report split across pages turns one report into several review groups, so the same identity question is asked more than once and one report's records look unrelated.
- **Receiving clinician:** a record merged across people, or one report shown as several, both misstate provenance.

## Where it stands

- **Clinical key (v1) ✅.** `clinicalSourceIdentityV1` (`src/server/intake-source-identity.ts:13-23`) hashes the issuing system and record ID, or else the original hash, locator and envelope ID. It leaves out report subject and package member. `INTAKE_SOURCE_IDENTITY_VERSION = 1` (`:11`), and the comment asks to keep the bytes stable until a versioned migration.
- **Candidate key (v1) ✅.** `candidateSourceIdentityV1` (`:25-40`) also binds the delivery (`file.id`) and, when present, the report member and full subject anchor, with different whitespace handling.
- **Report-group key ✅.** `src/server/intake-report-groups.ts` ~:70-83 hashes the original ID and hash, source system, member, the full report and subject anchors including their locators, and the proposed subject role. The locators are what split a running-header report across pages.
- **Refuse, never merge ✅.** Unsafe reuse of a clinical key across subjects or members returns `CLINICAL_SOURCE_SCOPE_COLLISION` (`src/server/clinical-source-scope.ts:17`; thrown at `src/server/clinical-import.ts:1458`) before any accepted write ([refuse unsafe clinical source reuse](../import/import-reconciliation.md#refuse-unsafe-clinical-source-reuse)). As a result, repeated reports in different ZIP members stay pending even when bytes and subject match, and cross-member consolidation remains off pending the equivalence contract and owner decision below.
- **No explanation of a split** is shown in the review UI (grooming report, 2026-09-30; not re-checked here).
- The current consumers a coordinated identity change must preserve are listed in `import-reconciliation.md` ("Persisted identity domains"): candidate and group versions, drafts, identity and source confirmations, acceptance and contribution receipts, clinical entity IDs and evidence links, and individual correction exceptions.

## Requirements

1. Write the current-format equivalence contract: what makes two occurrences the same clinical source, the same candidate and the same report, including whitespace, originals, member and subject scope. Equal values, dates or names never establish identity (P5).
2. Report grouping no longer splits a coherent running-header report across pages. Any split that remains is explained in review, naming what differs (member, subject, source system).
3. Apply the decided contract coherently to every current-format consumer listed above. Preserve current supported-format accepted history and exact scopes of identity confirmation, correction exceptions and relationship decisions through replay/cache rebuild. Add no old-schema decoder, archive migration or automatic reset; unsupported authority fails explicitly. Changing current matching policy never rewrites an earlier accepted decision or widens its authority.
4. Restore cross-member acceptance/consolidation only if the owner adopts it, after (1)–(3), and only for the same proven source and subject scope. Otherwise retain separate occurrences with a clear explanation; do not treat this unresolved decision as adopted.
5. Writes scale with what changed (`AGENTS.md`, [record versions](../data/record-version-storage.md#source-text-write-amplification)): any new identity/correction evidence uses bounded changed records or references; it does not rewrite whole journals.
6. Fictional-fixture tests: a running-header report across three pages forms one group; a family ZIP whose two members repeat an issuing ID for different people stays separate; the same report in two ZIP members for one person follows the adopted consolidate-or-keep-separate policy; current-format drafts, receipts, corrections, accepted versions and evidence reproduce after cache loss without widening earlier decisions.

## Owner decisions needed

- Whether cross-member consolidation should come back at all, or whether "kept separate, explained" is enough.
- No old-schema upgrade decision is needed for this release scope. The current-format rollout must preserve accepted authority; do not infer permission to reset an existing archive.
