## User Story

As a [Caregiver](../design/personas.md#file-family-records),
I want to clearly distinguish the people whose records I manage,
So that I can avoid choosing or reviewing the wrong person.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. I open one issue containing actual People screenshots and short walkthroughs, without having to create the example records myself.
2. I review Self and other people, including long or identical names, keyboard navigation and a narrow layout.
3. I inspect prescription and procedure summaries and comment in the issue with approval or what should change.
4. The builders address actionable feedback and update the same issue. Other backlog work continues while my response is pending.

## Agent-owned preparation

Include fictional prescriptions and procedures sufficient to judge the actual summaries. Do not invent product fields or redesign the app merely to stage the review.

Prepare and verify these flows:

- **Person distinction:** open People, distinguish Self from non-Self, select each alike-named person, inspect details and return. Show whose records are displayed and how long names and distinguishing details appear.
- **Keyboard navigation:** perform selection, detail and return using the keyboard, showing visible focus and recording actual focus order and outcomes. Screenshots alone do not prove keyboard behavior.
- **Narrow layout:** repeat the selection/detail journey at a recorded narrow viewport, checking labels, controls and overflow. Do not claim physical-phone qualification from viewport simulation.
- **Summary usefulness:** show each selected person's actual prescription and procedure summaries with sufficient surrounding ownership context and focused questions for the owner.

Run applicable existing People/person-scope, mounted and real-browser checks. Fix reproducible in-scope defects and preserve regressions before asking the owner to judge usability.
