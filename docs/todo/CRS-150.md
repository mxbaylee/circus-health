# 🗂️ CRS-150: Offer selective packets and a plain emergency summary

Status: open. Remainder of CRS-147 after the person-scoping, correction-provenance, brief-disclosure and real-PDF correctness work. This item concerns packet composition, not changing retained records or originals.

## Who and why

- **Low-effort self-tracker:** start with the right facts for an annual visit without selecting every record.
- **Caregiver:** prepare a managed person's packet without disclosing another person's records.
- **Privacy-sensitive patient:** omit a sensitive record deliberately, while disclosing that the packet is selective without revealing what was omitted.
- **Receiving clinician:** see a packet's scope and whether current medications and conditions are personal confirmations, source assertions, or absent from the selected packet.
- **Emergency contact:** receive a simple one-page printout they can read without app access.

The correctness foundation is documented in `src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md`. Provider packets currently include the person's normalized clinical entries, explicitly assigned raw clinical assertions and selected notes, with no category presets, per-record packet exclusion, packet-specific withholding disclosure or one-page emergency summary. Unassigned older raw assertions remain outside automatic packets with a generic ownership notice; [CRS-156](CRS-156.md) covers historical ownership restoration. Existing care roles and source assertions are not an inferred clinical problem list.

## Dependency sequence

1. **CRS-085 Conditions** must provide reviewed, person-scoped condition records and a separate personal current-status assertion before the annual and emergency presets can claim a current condition list. Until then, literal source assertions must remain labeled as such, and an absent list cannot mean “no conditions.” CRS-085's owner questions about status, links, visits and allergies remain decisions, not implemented facts.
2. **Allergies:** no accepted allergy entity exists today. Decide whether CRS-085 grows a parallel allergy record/status contract or a separate ticket supplies it. The annual and emergency presets may include literal, cited allergy assertions, labeled “recorded in source,” but cannot claim a confirmed allergy list or “no known allergies” from missing assertions. A named, reviewed personal allergy status is required if the preset is to use those claims.
3. **CRS-129 page approval:** packet evidence should carry the record's text-approval status once available. Packet composition must not silently turn an unapproved source into a confirmed fact. CRS-118's consumed-page dependency narrows which approvals matter.
4. Finish this ticket's presets and emergency summary, then run CRS-073's release-build print inspection on the actual PDF and evidence JSON.

## Requirements

1. Offer jobs as starting presets: annual visit with a new provider, narrow specialist visit, dentist, and optional one-page emergency summary. A move/checkpoint export belongs to CRS-148 and is not a print preset. Every preset starts from one selected person and preserves the single-subject guard.
2. The annual starting selection proposes family history, confirmed current conditions and medications, procedures/surgeries, recorded allergies, immunizations and recent tests. Moxie may draft a prose note from those selected facts, but the person reviews and approves the draft before printing. No unsupported interpretation or automatic “all clear” language.
3. Specialist and dentist selections are reviewable proposals. For a specialist, show proposed relevant conditions, medications and records; never silently suppress other records as irrelevant. For a dentist, begin with current medications, recorded allergies and relevant conditions, with the same review. Prior dental X-rays remain separate companion originals unless selected.
4. Categories must use knowable metadata: accepted record kind, an explicit time window and person-applied tags. Do not infer sensitivity, annual relevance or clinical significance from a model alone. Permit manual category and single-record inclusion/exclusion for each packet. A persistent per-record “always leave out of packets” preference overrides presets and can be changed by the profile user; it never alters the underlying record or the person's own app view.
5. If anything is omitted from a packet, print exactly “Some records were left out at the patient's request” or equivalently clear disclosure. Do not name or count excluded sensitive records in the PDF or companion evidence that is shared with that packet. The preview must make the actual inclusion and exclusion choices visible to the person, with a warning if the selection leaves a category empty.
6. The one-page emergency summary starts only after the correctness foundation and must include identity, personally confirmed current medication use, recorded allergies and confirmed current conditions with citations and uncertainty labels, selected emergency contacts, and who prepared it. It uses plain language and does not make clinical recommendations. It must fit a real Letter PDF page in automated rendering checks, or fail visibly rather than spill into an unreviewed second page.
7. Preset selection, exclusions, actor/approval metadata and the withheld disclosure must be reflected consistently in the preview, PDF, and evidence JSON. The snapshot fingerprint must cover each selection and preference so a stale preview cannot print changed choices. Avoid copying full record or source content into a journal entry on each preference change.
8. Test with fictional data for Self and managed people, mixed-person records, sensitive exclusions, empty categories, a changed exclusion between preview and download, and a one-page PDF whose extracted text and page count are checked. Verify that private excluded content is absent from both PDF and shareable evidence JSON.

## Design ideas to evaluate

- Keep a packet recipe as a small list of included record IDs and category predicates over stable record metadata; compute the actual membership once for a frozen preview. Show the count and titles privately in the review UI, but publish only included records and the generic withholding notice.
- Offer “recent tests” as a user-editable date window (for example, the last 12 months) plus manual choices. This is a proposed default, not an owner decision or a clinical definition of annual relevance.
- A specialist could start from a visit note or person-applied topic tag. Moxie can propose relevance with cited reasons, but the user confirms every narrowed selection before output.
- Store the durable “always leave out” flag as a compact personal preference keyed by record ID, with a journal entry for only the changed preference. Carry preferences through rebuild and export without changing source evidence.
- Let the emergency summary use a separate, constrained layout and page-count test while reusing the packet's subject, citations, correction labels and contact selection.
