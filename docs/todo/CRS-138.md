# CRS-138: Ask when a saved name differs from the printed name only by a suffix

Status: open. Remainder of CRS-137, implemented at `83a46db` and deleted there without an independent passing grade. Grading on 2026-09-30 gave B−. What shipped is described in [banner compatibility and report-scoped decisions](../import/identity-review.md#banner-compatibility-and-report-scoped-decisions): another report's receipt is not reused when two saved owners match the printed name, and a reused Self receipt is checked against Self's banner date. Line numbers below were reported at `e06bc78`; re-verify before editing.

## Decision and why

Medical-records practice treats generational suffixes and relatives who share a name as classic matching failures ([CRS-130](CRS-130.md#medical-records-practice)). A shared name must be a reason to ask, not to reuse.

- **Caregiver:** a parent and child saved as "Robin Lane Sr." and "Robin Lane Jr." upload one family export whose reports print only `Patient: Robin Lane`. Confirming one report as Self must not file the other into Self.
- **Low-effort self-tracker:** a name that is unique in the profile, with or without a suffix, still gets no new prompt.
- **Receiving clinician:** a Jr. record in Sr.'s packet is a wrong-person record.

## Where it stands

Canonical identity names keep Jr., Sr., II and III (test `canonical identity names retain Jr., Sr., II and III`). So after A is confirmed as Self, the printed "Robin Lane" becomes a Self alias; only Self matches it, the more-than-one-owner rule never fires, and B returns non-blocking `prior_confirmation`. Reproduced by the grader (scratch test `adv137.test.ts`, `GRADER R1–R4`, not in the repository). Controls behave correctly: a Jr. banner date blocks B; "Robin Lane" saved on Jr. too makes B ask; suffixed printed names match Jr.

## Requirements

A report's own receipt still resolves it. Enforce it in the shared policy (`src/server/intake-identity-policy.ts`, the reuse gate near `:888-893`) so preview, feed, individual and bulk acceptance agree.

3. Record in `identity-review.md` that suffixes are kept when names are compared and what follows from that.
4. A report's own Self receipt is recognized by `groupId` only, while its own Person receipt must also match `groupVersionId` (`:849-854`). Decide whether Self should match the version too; if so, change and test it (suspected, not reproduced: an older-version Self receipt bypasses the banner, unreadable-date and shared-name checks).
