3. Compare exact pending state, order, receipts, serialization/search and effective pins before/after restart, lock, interruption and total cache loss; compare original hashes and accepted records before/after reviewed acceptance, private copy and recovery. Backups must select coherent authority. Failure/retry coverage must include all publication boundaries, published-head/SQL-rollback conflicts and ambiguous completion, without duplicate receipts or lost decisions.
4. Attribute intake-batch-journal.ts separately: it currently scans/replays its history on every write. Carry any unresolved amplification into a scoped prerequisite/follow-up in CRS-198 before claiming bounded processing. Replacing this separate journal is not silently included here. Likewise disclose any remaining CPU full-view work and scope concrete follow-ups where needed.
5. Keep raw artifacts outside Git; publish portable fictional measurements and exact validation limitations in maintained storage/import/recovery documentation. No sparse clinical substitute, source omission, weaker oracle or helper-only measurement counts as a pass. Repoint remaining capacity work to implemented evidence after this ticket is independently graded.
