Earlier journal, projection and vault-index measurements do not qualify the combined intake path.

## User Story

As a [High-precision tracker](../design/personas.md#build-a-history),
I want to continue reviewing and accepting records as my history grows,
So that routine changes stay practical and my records remain correct.

## Plain-English User Walkthrough

1. I make review and acceptance decisions in a growing collection.
2. I reopen it after an interruption and check that the decisions and records I saved are still correct.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario: Measure real mutation growth

Given a fictional history is qualified at initial and 100, 200 and 300 changes,
When actual review and acceptance APIs mutate the history,
Then correct records are retained and actual durable writes, allocation, reads and host work are reported separately.

### Scenario: Recover at a publication failure

Given a real mutation encounters a staged, immutable-write, publication or ambiguous-completion failure,
When the profile is reopened or rebuilt,
Then acknowledged state survives and unacknowledged state is not falsely reported as accepted.

### Engineer context

Primitive-level tests do not prove the complete mutation path; this ticket measures actual writes, host work and recovery separately.

## Detailed engineering requirements

1. Use independently fictional fixtures for hundreds of real batch/proposal/review mutations at 100/200/300 checkpoints, then one small changed item after a large workflow. Exercise actual application APIs, including reviewed clinical acceptance; a small primitive payload alone is insufficient evidence.
2. Count all immutable record segments, intake contributions/heads/commits, transaction results/receipts, object counts, history rereads and SQLite allocation/contents/metadata/field references across source_files and app_meta, plus lookup/search projection contents and link rows. Separate initial-state cost, one-time raw-to-normalized conversion, existing-profile opening/reconstruction, and hydration/serialization/diff/hash/copy work. Search `dtoDetailsBytes` currently counts the physical compact source row for original intakes; measure reconstructed full-DTO work separately rather than treating that counter as full-envelope bytes. Demonstrate changed-evidence writes without repeated growing snapshots or whole reference lists in any durable or derived representation. Retain all evidence; no history pruning or runtime-capacity increase.
3. Compare exact pending state, order, receipts, serialization/search and effective pins before/after restart, lock, interruption and total cache loss; compare original hashes and accepted records before/after reviewed acceptance, private copy and recovery. Include the [consumer inventory](../import/intake-state-access.md#operational-consumer-inventory), stale-review/wrong-person refusals and manually created/duplicate records. Backups must select coherent authority. Failure/retry coverage must include all publication boundaries, published-head/SQL-rollback conflicts and ambiguous completion, without duplicate receipts or lost decisions.
4. Attribute intake-batch-journal.ts separately: it currently scans/replays its history on every write. Carry any unresolved amplification into a scoped prerequisite/follow-up in CRS-194 before claiming bounded processing. Replacing this separate journal is not silently included here. Likewise disclose any remaining CPU full-view work and scope concrete follow-ups where needed.
5. Keep raw artifacts outside Git; publish portable fictional measurements and exact validation limitations in maintained storage/import/recovery documentation. No sparse clinical substitute, source omission, weaker oracle or helper-only measurement counts as a pass. Repoint remaining capacity work to implemented evidence after this ticket is independently graded.

This ticket does not authorize paid inference or repeat the known-failing dense/heavy import just to finish. Broader unchanged-limit capacity, clinical completeness, diagnostic completeness, browser and adopted-route release checks remain open and need their own current evidence.
