# CRS-117: Incremental source-text writes

Status: open.

Source page mutations currently return the full source revision as the journal transaction result, even though page blobs are content addressed. Replace that repeated payload with compact durable receipts; readers can retrieve the referenced revision.

Avoid rehashing the entire original on every mutation and keep expensive IO off interactive paths. Preserve idempotent retries, durable recovery and public response compatibility. Verify journal growth per page edit stays bounded across small and large fictional documents. This consolidates reliability R9 and its growth-test requirement; it does not declare the defect fixed.

## Review findings, 2026-09-28 (R9)

`mutate()` returns `dto(revision)`, the whole revision including every page's text (`intake-source-text.ts:824`), and `record-versions.ts:553` stores it as `commit.result`. Fictional fixtures grew from 12.9 MB at 30 pages to 185.6 MB at 120 pages. Replace it with a receipt holding the revision id, changed page hashes and counts. Hash the original once at upload (`intake-source-text.ts:80-85`, `intake-files.ts:65`) rather than on every mutation, and move that IO off the synchronous path. The rule and the required linear-growth test are in [record versions](../data/record-version-storage.md#source-text-write-amplification); the owner decision is [CRS-126 D4](CRS-126.md#d4-storage-writes-scale-with-what-changed).

Do this before [CRS-086](CRS-086.md): parallel publishers would multiply the growth.
