# 🧬 CRS-205: Activate and qualify incremental intake authority

Status: open. Final focused implementation split from CRS-201; waits for [CRS-203](CRS-203.md) and [CRS-204](CRS-204.md). Before starting, confirm cutover plus verification still fits medium effort; split again if it does not. Unblocks the storage requalification retained in [CRS-194](CRS-194.md) and pending [PR #35](https://github.com/mxbaylee/circus-health/pull/35).

## Contract

Move operational intake state out of repeatedly versioned `source_files.details_json` into the transaction-backed changed-content store through the shared adapter. Originals and accepted medical records remain durable evidence. Model proposals still require human review before acceptance. Losing SQLite or an encrypted cache must reconstruct the same pending state and accepted authority from the existing durable record stream, without inference or a model call.

The owner requires no old-schema compatibility before production. Define the current representation explicitly and reject unsupported or missing authority; do not migrate, reset an archive or fabricate an empty workflow. Retain all changed-content evidence without pruning history to improve measurements.

## Acceptance

1. Switch initial registration and every operational writer to the new primitive, including proposal publication, workflow mutations, duplicate-review questions and report-acceptance receipts. Remove growing operational state/history from source-file details while preserving original identity, original downloads, source locator, reviewed source metadata, source-text pins and public intake/review contracts. Detect forbidden duplicated operational state rather than carrying a hidden second authority.
2. Preserve exact proposal, candidate, group, question, operation and plan-unit identities/status, effective version, discovery order, review fingerprints, acceptance receipts and source pins through restart, lock, interruption and total cache loss. Keep stale-review/source-pin conflicts and idempotent operation replay intact. Source confirmation, wrong-person refusals and manually created/duplicate records retain current semantics.
3. Back discovery ordering with a compact transactionally allocated scalar or equivalent index. Back acceptance replay lookup with an operation-to-intake/reference index. Avoid full-intake scans and growing reference/receipt arrays on each write. Index changes, operational contributions, source metadata updates, proposal registration and accepted clinical writes share the existing transaction boundary. Immutable accepted-record authority remains unchanged.
4. Qualify failure and retry ordering through the actual application: before staging, during immutable writes, before publication, after durable head publication but before SQLite commit, and ambiguous flush completion. Confirm acknowledged changes survive; candidates without a published durable head are invisible after rollback/reopen; an already published durable commit remains authoritative despite SQLite rollback or an exception; retries neither duplicate receipts nor lose review decisions. Corrupt, missing, cross-profile/source/hash or unsupported contributions fail explicitly. Confirm backups and cache-loss reconstruction select coherent source, intake and accepted-record state.
5. Use independently fictional fixtures for hundreds of real batch, proposal and review mutations at multiple sizes (include 100/200/300 checkpoints), then one small changed item after a large workflow. Count all immutable record segments, contribution/head/commit bytes, transaction results/receipts, object counts, history rereads and SQLite allocation/contents/metadata/field references across `source_files` and `app_meta`. Include initial-state cost separately. Demonstrate incremental changed-evidence writes and eliminate repeated growing workflow snapshots in durable authority and historical projection. Count hydration/diff/copy work separately; a small helper payload alone is insufficient evidence.
6. Compare exact pending state, order, receipts and version pins before/after restart and total cache loss; compare original hashes and accepted records before/after reviewed acceptance and recovery. Exercise actual batch/review/acceptance APIs and the consumer regressions from CRS-204. Retain raw artifacts outside Git and publish only portable fictional measurements. No runtime-limit increase, sparse clinical substitute, source omission or weaker oracle can count as a pass.
7. Update maintained storage, intake and recovery contracts, run formatting/type checks and focused tests, then full PR CI and independent grading. Delete this ticket on completion and repoint the remaining capacity investigation to implemented documentation. Do not repeat the known-failing heavy import merely to finish this ticket; broader unchanged-limit capacity, diagnostic completeness, browser and adopted-route qualifications remain open and require their own current evidence.

## Separate finding

`intake-batch-journal.ts` currently scans and replays its event history on every write. Attribute its rereads separately during the real mutation measurement and carry unresolved amplification into the broader investigation. Replacing that journal is not silently included in this cutover. The already implemented [conversation journal](../data/conversation-journal.md), [compact history projection](../data/record-version-storage.md#compact-history-projection) and [incremental encrypted index](../security/vault-index.md) do not prove this separate path is bounded.
