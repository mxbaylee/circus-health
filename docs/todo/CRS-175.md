# 🗂️ CRS-175: Condition packets and complete workflow qualification

Status: open. Packet and end-to-end remainder of CRS-085; depends on CRS-173's reviewed records, CRS-174's personal status/UI and [CRS-216](CRS-216.md)'s independently startable packet exclusion foundation. Unblocks CRS-150 annual/emergency current-condition lists. Conditions remain active product work by owner request, 2026-10-01.

Job: [Share a packet](../design/personas.md#share-a-packet), drawing on [Understand conditions](../design/personas.md#understand-conditions) and [Correct a record](../design/personas.md#correct-a-record). The receiving clinician needs a readable cited list; the emergency contact needs truthful current information; the privacy-sensitive patient needs control over disclosure; the caregiver and operator need person boundaries and reproducible evidence.

## User story

As a [Receiving clinician](../design/personas.md#share-a-packet), I want a printed packet to identify the patient, the basis for current condition status and any withheld or missing scope, so I can use it without assuming silence means no condition or allergy. Qualification must distinguish reviewed source evidence, personal status and disclosure choices across all packet outputs.

## Requirements

1. A packet problem list may call a condition current only after personal confirmation, never from provider active text. Carry exact source occurrence/citation, assigned person, literal provider status and separate personal assertion with actor/date. Preserve literal unconfirmed assertions as such; an absent list must never print “no conditions.”
2. Retain patient/caregiver correction and reclassification labels, original evidence and earlier accepted versions. Qualify person boundaries, per-record exclusions and honest withheld-record disclosure against CRS-216 and the persona jobs; CRS-150 later integrates job presets. A privacy-sensitive patient must be able to keep a selected sensitive condition out of one provider's packet without erasing its history.
3. Allergies remain a separate scope decision: no accepted allergy entity/status exists. Allocate a separately reviewed allergy record/status dependency, or explicitly adopt a parallel contract before a packet claims a confirmed allergy list or “no known allergies.” Safe interim packets may cite literal allergy assertions, labeled recorded in source; absence cannot establish no allergy. Do not infer personal allergy status from provider text. This question remains open, not an owner decision to build allergies in the Conditions slice.
4. Qualify the full workflow on independently fictional problem lists, FHIR Condition, visit summaries and relative records: review/acceptance, personal confirmation, original inspection, links, corrections, packets, exclusions, backup/restore and SQLite/cache-loss history. Equal mentions stay separate unless a relationship was explicitly reviewed. Visits remain notes, never a date book or a new entity.
5. Update NOTE-EXPORTS, intake/data model and maintained user procedures with implemented behavior and current limits. Get an independent grade against every remaining requirement, carry unresolved subsets into named tickets, and retire this item only when its scope is proved.

## Current evidence and launch scope

Grooming, 2026-10-02: [NOTE-EXPORTS](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md#two-simple-flows) explicitly says condition ingestion, personal status and packet summaries are unavailable. Conditions can remain in literal provider narratives; eligible unrepresented raw assertions require exact person evidence, and unprojected intake rows are not automatically printed as additional raw assertions ([historical raw assertion ownership](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md#historical-raw-assertion-ownership)). A stored condition or retained original therefore does not prove that a current-condition packet job is supported.

**Launch scope decision proposed:** retain CRS-150's required annual/emergency current-condition lists, or explicitly adopt a narrower launch packet whose limits are disclosed. Until a narrower scope is adopted, CRS-173 → CRS-174 → CRS-175 are production prerequisites of the existing production-marked CRS-150 requirement. Marking that chain is dependency accounting, not a new owner decision to expand the feature. The supported launch is the owner's household installation on localhost; this dependency does not create a remote-sharing or clinical-certification requirement.

## Workflow acceptance additions and unresolved policies

- Carry affirmative, uncertain, refuted/negated, historical and personal-disagreement cases from CRS-173/174 into both PDF and evidence JSON. Show qualifiers beside the relevant assertion, not only in an inaccessible raw appendix. A current list follows the selected personal-status contract; provider active text, source omission and an empty accepted list cannot establish “no conditions.” A reviewed association must not become an uncited causal diagnosis in the packet.
- Use CRS-174's selected longitudinal identity/confirmation contract for three distinct provider mentions, conflicting source statuses, re-exported history and later amendments. Include source occurrence references, personal actor/time, correction provenance and both current and earlier accepted evidence. A later provider version does not update personal status; use CRS-143's decided source-current/display policy without treating patient correction as provider amendment.
- Qualify exclusions across the PDF, evidence JSON, selected narratives and original/attachment companion list against CRS-216. A sensitive condition may also occur in a source shared by an included record. Use CRS-216's decided policy for withholding that companion, an explicit disclosed inclusion choice, or a separately labeled derivative; never alter the immutable original or claim exclusion while an offered companion discloses the condition. A withheld-record notice must not reveal the sensitive label or imply a complete problem list. CRS-216 owns that decision; this ticket supplies the Condition-specific cases rather than a second exclusion system.
- Test Self and caregiver packets, a correction moving an assertion to another person, unresolved source ownership, per-record exclusion and a stale preview after correction. The PDF and evidence companion must agree on subject, personal confirmation, qualifiers, omissions and citations. Qualify replay, backup/restore and SQLite/cache-loss through actual reviewed intake/acceptance, rather than direct table fixtures alone.
- Allergy scope remains requirement 3's open decision. A separately reviewed allergy/status dependency needs a named ticket and declared packet behavior if adopted; this ticket does not silently adopt it. Conditions or absence from imported lists cannot supply a confirmed allergy list.

The original rationale remains: a chronic-condition tracker needs longitudinal context, a receiving clinician needs a cited problem list, a low-effort self-tracker needs checkup preparation, and a privacy-sensitive patient needs disclosure control. Personal status, resolution dates, links and visit tags remain CRS-174 decisions; literal code/vocabulary matching remains CRS-173. Recommendations here are not additional owner-adopted policy.
