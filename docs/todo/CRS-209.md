## Contract

Move operational intake state out of repeatedly versioned source_files.details_json through the shared access adapter. Originals and accepted medical records remain durable evidence. Human review is still required before accepting model proposals. Reconstruction after total cache loss must require no inference. The owner requires no old-schema compatibility before production: reject unsupported, missing or duplicated authority; never reset an archive or fabricate an empty workflow.
