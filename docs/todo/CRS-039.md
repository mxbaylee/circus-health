# CRS-039: Productive automatic continuation and bounded large PDFs

Status: open.

Continue productive reading through long files without an arbitrary document-wide page, step or elapsed-time stop. Keep per-operation resource limits, explicit Stop and authorization boundaries. Use observable progress and checkpoints to recover stalled work, retain completed proposals, and isolate unreadable pages.

Retry transient provider limits and interrupted extraction automatically with provider deadlines and backoff. Recover running jobs after restart when authorized unlock occurs; start accepted uploads on the server rather than relying on an open tab. Reconcile unknown provider outcomes where possible; preserve possibly billed attempts and prevent duplicate local publication on retry. A wait is not successful completion.

Cover failed-page retry, human transcription resolving extraction issues, capacity overflow as located exceptions, and progress wording. Lock/logout authority is separate in [CRS-081](CRS-081.md). Former reliability feedback R1–R7, R34, R37–R38 and R45 belongs here.

## Owner decisions

[CRS-126 D2](CRS-126.md#d2-no-size-or-time-limits-on-reading-detect-stuckness-instead) and [D3](CRS-126.md#d3-waits-are-backoffs-never-pauses): no size or time limit on reading, stuckness means no progress for a period, and waits are backoffs. The person never needs Resume except after **Stop imports** or to fix something only they can, such as provider sign-in.

## Review findings, 2026-09-28

Line numbers refer to `2ae9a0f`.

- **R1. An unknown provider outcome is a dead end.** `intake-model-attempts.ts:157-162,190-196` records it; `INTAKE_ATTEMPT_UNRESOLVED` then blocks every later request (`proxy-model-bridge.ts:1244`, `assistant.ts:2956-2960`, `intake-batches.ts:1159-1165`). No reconcile path exists. Direction: mark the attempt possibly billed, back off, and retry with a new request identity; keep the unknown attempt in the ledger; prevent duplicate local publication.
- **R2. Backoff has an attempt cap.** `safeRetry` (`intake-batches.ts:377-381`) stops after five attempts. Use exponential backoff with jitter, no attempt cap, and honor `Retry-After`. Only named non-retryable classes become exceptions: authentication, policy refusal, malformed request. A 429 or 503 alone never does.
- **R3. Interrupted extraction waits on the person** (`intake-source-extraction-operation.ts:254-265,302-335,372-389`; `intake-batches.ts:539`) and says "Source text needs your review", which is untrue. Retry from the page checkpoint and say it is retrying.
- **R4. Replace `AUTOMATIC_SOURCE_CAPTURE_LIMITS`** (`{steps: 10, elapsedMs: 120_000}`, `intake-batches.ts:76,503-509`) with a monotonic progress counter and a watchdog: on no progress for the threshold, cancel and retry from the checkpoint; only repeated stalls on one step become a located exception.
- **R5. Resume running batches on unlock after a restart** (`intake-batches.ts:256-266,1214`).
- **R6. Retry failed pages automatically,** and let a confirmed transcription close an OCR-unavailable issue (`intake-source-extraction.ts:272-295`, `intake-source-ocr.ts:294-306`, `intake-source-text.ts:996,1070-1076`).
- **R7. The 10,000-page / 32 MB evidence ceiling** (`intake-source-text.ts:145-152`) becomes a located exception on the pages beyond it, not a rejection. Reconsider whether it is needed after [CRS-117](CRS-117.md).
- **R34. Progress wording** (`ImportReadingActivity.tsx:43`): "Done" and "Done, with exceptions"; an estimate in hours for long files; "Estimating…" before a rate exists; pages read; a combined estimate across files.
- **R37. Start accepted uploads on the server** (`ImportPage.tsx:1501`), not from the open tab.
- **R38. Stop imports is the only person-facing stop** (`intake-batches.ts:691`); every other stop is a backoff or a located exception.
- **R45. Tests:** a stall-detection test and a large-document run past the old ten-step / 120-second limit; see [CRS-122](CRS-122.md).

## Open questions

- A retry after an unknown outcome may bill twice. D3 requires the retry; decide whether that cost is accepted or whether the route offers idempotency or late-result recovery. The retired implementation specification preferred reconciliation before resending.
- The stall threshold. Proposed start: a multiple of the slowest legitimate step (raster timeout 75 s, OCR timeout 60 s), not a document-level budget.
