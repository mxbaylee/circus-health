# People, jobs and current product limits

Reviewed against source and test definitions on 2026-10-02. This is the maintained persona reference, organized by jobs rather than screens. ✅ means the cited implementation or test establishes the narrow statement; it does not mean a fresh live-provider, physical-device or usability run passed. ⚠️ marks an unverified journey or unmet need, with the ticket and evidence needed to settle it. Open specifications and proposed policy remain in [the backlog](../todo/readme.md); this document describes known needs, implemented boundaries and current limits.

## Personas and evidence

Prompt tolerance, frequency and failure costs below are product inferences unless explicitly attributed to the owner. They are design hypotheses, not research interviews or new authorization rules.

- **Low-effort self-tracker.** Inferred from import review. Uploads occasional results and visit documents; wants few, batchable questions. Owner, 2026-09-30: the goal is a successful checkup with accurate data. Repeated prompts cost attention; missed or misfiled records undermine that goal. Uses [file and review](#file-and-review), [build a history](#build-a-history) and [share a packet](#share-a-packet).
- **Caregiver.** Inferred from People and family import scenarios. Owner, 2026-09-30: managed people have no logins, profiles are never shared, and the caregiver prints the patient's PDF for others. Uploads family records as they arrive, sometimes in separate profiles; tolerates ownership questions when evidence is ambiguous, not repeated questions for one reviewed report. Wrong-person acceptance is a safety failure. Needs [family filing](#file-family-records), [correction](#correct-a-record) and [sharing](#share-a-packet); the caregiver is the reviewing actor, not the managed person.
- **Records assembler.** Inferred from evidence review. Collects a burst of documents for a clinician, second opinion or dispute. Will inspect source details, but needs to distinguish source text, machine reading and human approval. Missing provenance makes the packet unreliable. Uses [file and review](#file-and-review), [correction](#correct-a-record) and [sharing](#share-a-packet).
- **High-precision tracker.** Owner, 2026-09-29: imports a backlog from about fifteen sources, including eyeglass prescriptions, then each new record; may re-upload a whole history to obtain one new note. Needs exact values and low repeated work. Inferred tolerance: detailed review of differences, little tolerance for re-answering unchanged questions. Silent omissions, false duplicates and unexpected repeated inference cost trust and money. Uses [build a history](#build-a-history), [unusual evidence](#read-unusual-evidence), [trends](#follow-values-and-medications) and [operation](#operate-and-recover).
- **Receiving clinician.** Owner, 2026-09-30: receives PDF plus evidence JSON and never opens the app. Needs whose record it is, what was checked, missing scope and corrections with actor/time/source. Does not upload or answer app prompts; unclear provenance and wrong-person data are safety failures. Uses [sharing](#share-a-packet) and [correction](#correct-a-record).
- **Operator.** Inferred role accepted for inclusion by the owner, 2026-09-30. Runs a small laptop installation and pays for AI reading. Owner, 2026-10-01: medical-grade record integrity is required, as defined [below](#record-integrity-lens). Inferred jobs include installation, provider configuration, backups, restoration, updates and incident diagnosis; occasional setup and ongoing backup work need plain-language instructions. Losing evidence or misreading a failed save has a high cost. Uses [operate and recover](#operate-and-recover). This is a household responsibility, not a separate authenticated administrator role; operating the installation does not establish clinical approval.
- **Privacy-sensitive patient.** Inferred role accepted for inclusion, 2026-09-30. Upload cadence varies; needs selective sharing of sensitive records without losing their own history. Will review disclosure choices; one accidental disclosure can defeat the whole job. Uses [sharing](#share-a-packet), [family filing](#file-family-records) and [operation](#operate-and-recover).
- **Chronic-condition tracker.** Inferred role accepted for inclusion, 2026-09-30. May follow only one or two values over years. Needs comparable units, source dates and an honest distinction between a documented condition and personal current status. Tolerates meaningful confirmation, not repeated identical mentions. False trends or inferred diagnoses are costly. Uses [trends](#follow-values-and-medications) and [conditions](#understand-conditions).
- **Emergency contact.** Inferred role accepted for inclusion, 2026-09-30. Reads in a hurry, does not upload or maintain records, and has no app login. Cannot resolve app prompts; stale or misleading current-status claims are dangerous. Uses the printed [sharing](#share-a-packet) boundary. Direct emergency access remains an open option, not a shipped capability.

## File and review

Serves the Self-tracker, Records assembler and Caregiver. Covers former import scenarios **1 (first file), 3 (small delta), 11 (missing/partial dates)** and the **complete-text acceptance scenario**.

- ✅ Proposals and clinical acceptance are separate; unsupported/uncertain subjects remain raw, and stale review cannot partly import. Evidence: `src/server/test/clinical-import.test.ts`, tests “uncertain subjects and unsupported content remain raw; safe mappings persist and reapply” and “stale review and invalid edits cannot partly import; rules cannot reuse patient values.”
- ✅ A name-only match is selectable but remains a reviewable guess, distinct from verified DOB or human confirmation. Evidence: `src/server/test/intake-generic-name-question.test.ts`, “unlabelled banner name-only … matches are selectable and bulk accept without confirmation”; [identity review](../import/identity-review.md#automatic-matches-and-human-confirmation) documents “(you?) · Review” versus “Change.” A matching name alone does not prove identity.
- ✅ Identical-upload reuse currently requires the same acquisition provider, original hash and original filename (`src/server/intake.ts`, `uploadIntake` lookup of `intake_original`). Renaming or changing delivery can create another intake. Reviewed clinical reuse is separate: `src/server/test/clinical-import.test.ts`, “identical unscoped copies add attribution but changed unscoped deliveries refuse reuse.”
- ⚠️ The complete low-effort journey—automatic reading, exact page/location, no unnecessary question for a name/DOB match, filtered bulk review excluding conflicts, counted receipts and clear saved destination—needs [CRS-190](../todo/CRS-190.md) journey evidence and [CRS-062](../todo/readme.md#crs-062) real-route qualification. Broader renamed-file recognition is an unresolved product choice in [CRS-126](../todo/CRS-126.md), not a blanket duplicate guarantee.
- ⚠️ Full approved-date behavior, visible partial dates and uncertain centuries remain [CRS-121](../todo/readme.md#crs-121); Conditions' unknown dates conflict with the all-records full-date rule and need an explicit decision in [CRS-173](../todo/CRS-173.md). A guessed date is not source evidence.
- ⚠️ Forty pages with six clinical findings still need forty pages of retained readable text, including administrative text and legible handwriting; located unreadability must survive reload/recovery without a new model reading. [CRS-115](../todo/CRS-115.md) owns qualification. [CRS-129](../todo/CRS-129.md) owns page-text attestation and bulk approval of listed pages; accepted records do not yet establish that their text was attested. [CRS-116](../todo/CRS-116.md) owns missing-record and “no clinical record needed” review. Show pages read, text retained, proposals, saved records and outstanding work separately.

## Build a history

Serves the High-precision tracker and Self-tracker. Covers scenarios **2 (whole history), 4 (annual re-export), 9 (interruption)** and the **structured portal export** job, including migration from another app as a job rather than a new persona.

- ✅ Current processing records model-pass capture baselines, while storage and lookup mechanisms retain originals and accepted history; see [processing](../import/processing.md) and [intake state access](../import/intake-state-access.md). These mechanisms do not establish complete large-import performance.
- ⚠️ Full histories still need every page accounted for, a review date filter that never skips reading, one-action clearing of reviewed matches, only actual changes left for review, truthful estimates and no duplicate or lost proposals after recovery. [CRS-086](../todo/CRS-086.md), [CRS-194](../todo/CRS-194.md), [CRS-140](../todo/CRS-140.md) and [CRS-121](../todo/readme.md#crs-121) retain those checks. Count requests, retries, rereads, bytes and changed writes; fixture generation alone does not prove clinical completeness.
- ⚠️ Tab close, sign-out and profile switching must preserve authorized work and explicit Stop/Resume intent; the complete joint journey remains [CRS-081](../todo/CRS-081.md). Show progress, stalls from missing progress, measured-rate estimates and paid retry uncertainty; no app usage cap is adopted.
- ⚠️ FHIR/C-CDA exports need a named version/subset, patient/reference boundaries, unsupported-content accounting, repeated-history/amendment cases and original retention. A JSON file accepted as an upload or a direct-SQL FHIR-shaped fixture proves neither format's clinical ingestion. [CRS-126](../todo/CRS-126.md), [CRS-068](../todo/readme.md#crs-068) and [CRS-173](../todo/CRS-173.md) own that qualification. Unknown external references must not select a patient or fetch private data implicitly.
- ⚠️ Re-uploaded page approvals at corpus scale remain [CRS-129](../todo/CRS-129.md): measure unapproved pages, prompts and reused proof; never silently extend an attestation to changed content. Show expected work and unavailable cost estimates before initiation without inventing provider billing precision.

## File family records

Serves the Caregiver and Privacy-sensitive patient. Covers scenarios **6 (wrong person's file), 7 (changed name), 10 (relative's result), 14 (managed people)** and the **mixed-family archive** job.

- ✅ Report confirmations cannot cross originals or choose between competing owners. Evidence: `src/server/test/intake-identity-boundaries.test.ts`, “confirmation cannot cross an original even when the printed subject is unchanged” and “a same-named Person keeps another report from borrowing the Self receipt without a banner.” [Identity review](../import/identity-review.md) documents explicit Self/existing/new Person choices and confirmed-name reuse.
- ⚠️ A mixed-family ZIP with repeated issuing IDs, shared names, different members and running headers needs end-to-end review and separate accepted people, without widening one report's confirmation. [CRS-144](../todo/CRS-144.md), [CRS-139](../todo/CRS-139.md) and [CRS-068](../todo/readme.md#crs-068) retain grouping, explanation and qualification. Name changes may reuse a valid confirmation, never suppress a new conflict.
- ⚠️ Verify that a mismatch never rejects the retained original, family records stay outside Self's views/trends, relationship is optional, and switching profiles neither leaks data nor pauses authorized work. Current narrow identity tests do not close the complete [CRS-081](../todo/CRS-081.md)/[CRS-124](../todo/readme.md#crs-124) caregiver journey. Family-history mentions inside Self's report are a separate subject problem in [CRS-173](../todo/CRS-173.md).

## Read unusual evidence

Serves the High-precision tracker and Records assembler. Covers scenarios **8 (odd files), 12 (handwriting/vitals photo)** and the **imaging report with image companions** job.

- ✅ Host eligibility keeps DICOM and audio/video retain-only (`src/shared/intake-source-policy.ts`, `isRetainOnlyIntake`). This verifies the policy function, not every delivery route or companion relationship.
- ⚠️ HEIC/multipage TIFF reading is [CRS-127](../todo/readme.md#crs-127). Handwriting, a clinician note appearing in Notes and Documents, and photo measurements becoming cited test results need [CRS-115](../todo/CRS-115.md)/[CRS-062](../todo/readme.md#crs-062) fixtures with located uncertainty.
- ⚠️ An imaging report and its DICOM/image companions need distinct read-versus-retained outcomes, stable links, correct person assignment and reviewable clinical proposals from the report alone. [CRS-068](../todo/readme.md#crs-068) owns package qualification; DICOM interpretation remains [CRS-082](../todo/readme.md#crs-082), outside the current supported reading claim.
- ✅ Eyewear prescriptions map to documents with an opticalPrescription structure, distinct from medications and measured eye findings (`src/server/clinical-instructions.ts`; `src/server/test/clinical-person-ownership.test.ts`, “historical and optical documents use durable import ownership and default to Self”; `src/server/test/vision-prescriptions.test.ts`, “reviewed literal optical occurrences, corrections and indexed history survive encrypted cache loss”). ⚠️ Lossless end-to-end provider/rendered review still needs [CRS-062](../todo/readme.md#crs-062): sphere/cylinder/axis, side, literal units, original inspection and unsupported fields. A verified destination does not establish every optical export's completeness.

## Follow values and medications

Serves the Chronic-condition tracker and High-precision tracker. Covers scenario **13 (disagreeing medication list)** and the trends portion of scenario **4**.

- ✅ Shared comparison semantics and literal-value limits are documented in [measurement comparison](../features/measurement-comparison.md); source values remain distinct from derived conversions.
- ⚠️ [CRS-121](../todo/readme.md#crs-121) owns the complete cross-lab job: checked vocabulary matches, Hgb versus HbA1c negative controls, marked conversion at source precision, same analyte across years, unresolved units kept separate and reversible rules. A chart alone does not establish clinical equivalence.
- ⚠️ The same ticket owns additive medication imports: history never silently changes personal current use, omission never stops a medication, explicit stopped text offers a suggestion, and brand/generic matching uses the specified offline vocabulary. Test Self and caregiver confirmation separately. A document's list is not the person's current list.

## Understand conditions

Serves the Chronic-condition tracker, Self-tracker, Caregiver and Receiving clinician.

- ✅ Literal condition occurrence storage exists (`src/server/migrations/007-condition-occurrences.sql`; `src/server/test/condition-storage.test.ts`). Review kinds exclude Conditions (`src/shared/intake.ts`, `ReviewRecordKind`); direct-SQL recovery tests establish storage only.
- ✅ Current instructions prohibit invented diagnoses/causal relationships (`src/server/assistant-instructions.md`, clinical interpretation constraints; `src/server/clinical-instructions.ts`). The app has no accepted feature for diagnosing from abnormal labs or medication use.
- ⚠️ [CRS-173](../todo/CRS-173.md) owns reviewed extraction of explicitly documented conditions and negative controls for negation, uncertainty, history, education and another person's condition. [CRS-174](../todo/CRS-174.md) owns the unresolved longitudinal entity, personal status, links and UI. [CRS-175](../todo/CRS-175.md) owns packet qualification. No personally confirmed condition list or inferred-diagnosis feature is established today. Multiple literal mentions must not silently inherit one personal confirmation.

## Correct a record

Serves the Caregiver, Records assembler and Receiving clinician. Covers scenario **5 (corrected report)** and wrong-person remediation.

- ✅ Ownership corrections preserve evidence and are labelled as profile-user assertions rather than provider amendments; [change history](../data/change-history.md#ownership-evidence-and-correction-scope) and [note exports](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md) describe actor/time/reason and later-packet labels. Original bytes remain unchanged.
- ⚠️ Later-issued provider amendments becoming current regardless of upload order conflict with today's explicit relationship review. [CRS-143](../todo/CRS-143.md) retains that decision, old/new chart display and tests. Do not infer that a later upload wins.
- ⚠️ [CRS-139](../todo/CRS-139.md)/[CRS-141](../todo/CRS-141.md) own an understandable correction journey that preserves unaffected contributions, stale-review safety and interrupted saves. Verify provider amendment versus personal correction labels in a rendered packet. Earlier packet membership/delivery is not retained, so the app cannot identify everyone who received a misfiled fact; recipients and refreshed copies require explicit operator knowledge.

## Share a packet

Serves the Receiving clinician, Records assembler, Caregiver, Privacy-sensitive patient and Emergency contact.

- ✅ Person-scoped PDF plus evidence JSON, separate original companions and explicit partial report counts exist; `src/server/test/note-export-report-review.test.ts`, “provider PDF source and evidence count saved report items, then refresh after the rest are accepted”; [note exports](../../src/server/NOTE-EXPORTS.md) documents unread-source disclosure, owner corrections and mixed-owner refusal. Those counts describe source review, not packet membership or complete reading.
- ⚠️ Per-record privacy exclusions remain [CRS-216](../todo/CRS-216.md); job presets and a one-page emergency summary remain [CRS-150](../todo/CRS-150.md). Verify omissions across PDF, JSON, note text and companion originals without disclosing hidden names/counts. Printed review is [CRS-073](../todo/readme.md#crs-073); no current visual audit is implied.
- ⚠️ Current Conditions need [CRS-175](../todo/CRS-175.md); a confirmed allergy list has no accepted entity contract. Missing assertions cannot mean “no conditions” or “no known allergies.” The allergy decision remains there rather than being silently treated as another medication.
- ✅ Managed people/contact tags do not create logins ([People](../features/people-tags.md), [profile encryption](../security/profile-encryption.md)). Emergency contacts currently rely on material the patient shares. ⚠️ Direct emergency access is only [CRS-145](../todo/CRS-145.md); qualify print readability and freshness without assuming app access.

## Operate and recover

Serves the Operator, Caregiver and Privacy-sensitive patient.

- ✅ Supported setup is npm → Docker Compose → LiteLLM ([installation](../setup/installation.md)); profile recovery kits unlock encrypted archives but are not record backups ([profile encryption](../security/profile-encryption.md)). Originals/accepted versions are authority; SQLite is rebuildable ([profile storage](../data/profile-storage-and-rebuild.md)).
- ⚠️ Supported backup-command clarity, independent restore and safe update/refusal are [CRS-213](../todo/CRS-213.md), [CRS-214](../todo/CRS-214.md) and [CRS-215](../todo/CRS-215.md). A cache rebuild does not prove a separate backup can recover the installation. Retention/location choices remain operator choices; unsupported old formats require safe refusal, not silent reset.
- ⚠️ Device/drive/provider qualification remains [CRS-163](../todo/CRS-163.md), [CRS-152](../todo/CRS-152.md) and the [release checks](../todo/readme.md#release-and-usability-checks). Localhost is the adopted scope; hosting is conditional. Small-laptop suitability is measured by work counts and foreground responsiveness, not a fixed model-time bound.
- ⚠️ [CRS-190](../todo/CRS-190.md) owns novice incident usability: distinguish archive-full, cache-full, provider failures, corrupted evidence and a save with an uncertain outcome; explain retained originals, possible durable acceptance and safe next actions. [CRS-084](../todo/CRS-084.md) retains compromise/backup security questions. [CRS-140](../todo/CRS-140.md) retains retry/Stop visibility; provider billing or retention must not be guessed. Clinical audit history and sign-in monitoring are different capabilities.

## Record-integrity lens

Owner requirement, 2026-10-01: medical-grade means correct patient attribution, immutable originals, append-only accepted corrections with actor/time/source, explicit incomplete or uncertain scope, review before model-proposed acceptance, and recovery reproducing accepted records. It is not a claim of certification. A household operator and a receiving clinician share the need without sharing an account.

The implementation's provenance model is described in [record versions](../data/record-version-storage.md). As external design context, [HL7 FHIR R4 v4.0.1 Provenance](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/provenance.html) distinguishes the activity, agents and entities behind a resource version; version identity matters when describing a particular earlier fact. [FHIR R4 Condition](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/condition.html) distinguishes clinical status, verification status and different date roles. These references were checked on 2026-10-02; they do not establish FHIR ingestion, choose personal-status policy or impose provider regulation on this app.

Current identity safeguards address ambiguous names and generational suffixes; current correction documentation distinguishes a user's assertion from a provider amendment. Cross-report identity policy, delivery remediation and condition status still have open decisions in the linked tickets. No remembered external legal or clinical standard is treated as an implemented guarantee.
