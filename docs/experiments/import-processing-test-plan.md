# Proposed decisive tests for a composed import strategy

This is a proposed experimental specification, not executed work or permission to call a provider. Read the current import specification as the requirement; retain the historical results without changing their criteria. All three finalized independent designs prefer a durable text layer followed by bounded clinical reading and document-level assembly or dependency resolution. Their agreement establishes plausible components, not measured superiority. The owner expects adapters and useful compositions, not a single architecture winner. Full text retention is compatible with a whole-document clinical reader and with sparse clinical output.

## Component contracts and composition claims

**Prospective extension:** the owner's subsequent [human-assisted completion direction](import-processing-practical-strategy.md)
adds a separate source-review candidate. Scripted correction tests qualify lineage and workflow mechanics;
they cannot establish human error discovery, transcription accuracy or effort. Declare new criteria before
execution and retain the automatic-only grades. Review attestation and independently measured source accuracy
are separate endpoints.

The subsequent [detector-composition study](import-processing-detection-experiments.md) tests that
missing prerequisite: selecting repair targets without access to the answer key, then measuring
whole-document errors after scope-limited perfect repair and the source material exposed for review.

Keep four reports: (1) complete durable source text, (2) clinical facts and relationships, (3) scheduling, review and recovery, and (4) efficiency. A model can extract six correct findings while omitting readable text, retain every word while misattributing a finding, or pass both but lose a proposal after a crash. No aggregate score should hide any of these failures.

The stable composition is **receive/inventory → identity policy → full-text extraction → durable text adapter → clinical reader → context assembly/validation → review/acceptance**, with a scheduler and recovery contract around every stage. Some stages may overlap or be combined internally, but their observable evidence and costs remain separate. A structured export may supply facts without AI; this is an adapter route, not permission to skip source retention or review.

An adapter is eligible only when its declared contract preserves the information its consumer needs. Proposed component contracts and compatibility tests are:

| Boundary                        | Required observable contract                                                                                    | Discriminating compatibility test                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Original/format to inventory    | Exact checksum, source/member/page IDs, retain-only exclusion, supported/unreadable dispositions                | Equivalent renamed files decode identically; malformed member isolated; excluded imaging/video/audio never reaches AI                                              |
| Extractor to durable text       | Versioned literal spans, page/region coordinates, order/table links, uncertainty, visual-inspection disposition | Missing table header, transformed coordinates, absent annotation, or ambiguous alternative remains detectable after normalization; adapter cannot invent certainty |
| Durable text to reader          | Authorized source access, stable versioned references, image-region access, subject/review status               | Both W and B resolve the same source support; reformatting storage neither changes meaning nor leaks another profile                                               |
| Reader to assembly              | Candidate assertions plus source dependencies and unresolved work; every clinical scope has a disposition       | Equivalent compound/document assertions normalize equally; missing remote qualifiers are not supplied by the adapter                                               |
| Assembly to review              | Versioned proposals, supported fields, conflicts/questions, correct identity/date eligibility                   | Multi-page evidence accepted when valid; incomplete identity/date never becomes bulk-eligible; late dependency invalidates stale approval                          |
| Any stage to scheduler/recovery | Stable task/revision key, atomic commit, attempt ledger, waiting/exception distinctions                         | Retry neither loses committed output nor duplicates it; recomputation after changed evidence invalidates affected consumers                                        |

**Minimal coherent baseline composition C0:** one contract-conformant extractor path with native text plus visual inspection and located fallback; a durable text/provenance adapter; a deterministic clinical router selecting W for a supported whole-document scope and B with document assembly for scopes that require bounded processing; common source/identity validation and review; concurrency one. The fit/eligibility checks must be frozen from measured request size and supported capabilities, never inferred from gold quality or an arbitrary page count. This is a proposed baseline, not a claim that either route is already adequate. Both selected routes need evidence before C0 can claim general support. Until then unsupported strata remain explicit exceptions or experimental, not silently routed to an untested “best” path.

Meaningful incremental options are (E1) another extraction component under the same adapter, (G1) explicit dependency-graph assembly instead of lighter context assembly for remote relationships, and (A1) a persistent document actor with a durable passage ledger/notebook rather than B's explicit semantic handoffs. The third designer supplies A1 as a serious contrary component, not a fourth independent design. Its notebook is not the full-text archive; coverage and recovery requirements remain identical. Test A1 on H1/H2 only if B/W leave a demonstrated gap or orchestration cost question; this is at most two paired follow-up jobs against existing fixture baselines, labelled post-hoc regression evidence, with a new reserved fixture for a positive repaired-route claim. Do not claim a dependency graph is superior merely because it makes failures easier to count.

**Router and fallback policies are tested components, not unexplained glue.** Before execution, declare observable predicates, component versions, reasons, and next states. Typical predicates are actual format, native/rendered discrepancy, layout support, request payload fit, missing source dependency and invalid output; confidence alone cannot certify completeness. A policy may try a better visual extractor on unexplained regions, escalate unresolved clinical scope to W when it fits, use B/G1 when whole-document input is unsupported, or produce a located exception when no eligible composition resolves the issue. Every fallback preserves prior output, source/revision lineage and cumulative costs. Do not loop indefinitely or silently lower clinical/text requirements. Provider quota waiting is not an extraction-quality fallback.

Use **six proposed offline routing boundary cases** before live work: (1) native content with no discrepancy; (2) the same format with an image-only annotation; (3) just below a measured W input-fit boundary; (4) just above it; (5) unresolved remote dependency after the first clinical pass; (6) exhausted eligible fallback paths. Drive them with fake component receipts, not gold answers embedded in routing logic. Assert the declared component path, every-page coverage, final known/unknown status, no repeat cycle, all attempt costs and recoverability after a branch change. These prove policy implementation for those cases, not the quality of the fake components.

Evaluate normal routed composition and forced component interchanges separately. A forced W/B pair reveals topology effects; normal C0 execution reveals misrouting, fallback cost and integration errors. Report per-stratum eligibility and the actual selected path, including failures, rather than a pooled “winner” or picking the successful route after seeing gold. The compatibility sample is these edges and failure interactions, not the Cartesian product of every extractor, topology, unit size and concurrency. In particular, feed the same page text through an adapter with table/header links and a deliberately defective adapter that flattens them: the latter must be rejected or explicitly marked unsupported, never silently treated as an equivalent input. This local seam check needs no new provider job.

The primary component interchange changes **clinical context topology only**, holding source documents, extraction artifacts, model/route, prompts' substantive task, available evidence, review contract, output allowances and concurrency constant:

- **B — Bounded reading plus assembly:** structure-based scopes with verbatim supporting context, source-bound identity/date/header references, explicit unresolved dependencies and a document-level assembly/audit. Freeze scope construction and escalation rules before heldout work.
- **W — Whole-document clinical reading:** the same retained transcript and necessary source images in original order, with full-document clinical interpretation and the same deterministic validators and review boundaries. Do not require the model to copy complete text into clinical output. A nominal context window is not evidence the document fits reliably; actual rejected/truncated input is an outcome.

Use a shared durable evidence contract so a storage difference does not masquerade as a context advantage. A persistent agent that fetches passages is **not** the W arm; if tested as A1, label it a separate traversal topology with its own coverage policy. Compare B’s explicit unresolved-dependency propagation with A1’s document notebook/visit ledger on the same remote qualifier and late amendment, including restart between the early evidence and late reference. Score whether the correct support survives and is retrieved, not whether either internal representation resembles the oracle. Use fake transport for restart equivalence first; the conditional two-job A1 probe measures semantics separately. Do not introduce concurrency, a new OCR engine, new clinical schema, caching policy or an unrelated quality-assurance pass only in the preferred arm. B’s context assembly/dependency reconciliation is an inherent part of B and its full cost belongs to B; W need not receive a dummy assembly pass. Both compositions receive the same independent endpoint scoring and the same specified deterministic validation/review safeguards. If a model-based quality-audit component is included, expose it as a shared optional component with a declared policy, or compare it as a separate interchange rather than quietly advantaging one arm. Equal access means both can receive the supporting image/text; it does not mean their payload bytes or number of calls must be equal.

The exact object schema is not the clinical oracle. Before reading heldout outputs, freeze an adapter from each candidate's durable representation to a neutral evidence graph: source assertion, subject/role, field values and modifiers, date roles, source-event identifiers, and supporting locations/relations. A report object with six fully represented findings can equal six separate records if both support the same required product actions. A text archive with no required structured test/medication fields cannot acquire those fields by scorer inference or a new AI pass. The adapter must report absent fields, preserve all candidate assertions, and never use gold answers to repair output. It may resolve candidate-authored references and normalize equivalent representations; it must not infer missing patient/date/header/value relationships from the transcript or gold.

## Stage 0 — Freeze contracts and test the scorer, no provider

Reuse the app's original/hash/page readers, proposal histories and report-context machinery in isolated prototypes where their contracts fit. Do not build a parallel production framework. Record current behavior separately from the proposed architecture. The sign-out/background contract is a proposed security change tracked by CRS-081; current locking/session behavior is not authorization to bypass it.

Create a source oracle independently from runtime code. It contains rendered-page truth, literal readable spans, meaningful order/table relations, clinical assertions, patient/date/specimen/report relationships, intended exceptions and original/page/region references. Runtime receives only ordinary files, authorized profile inputs and allowed user decisions, never fixture IDs carrying answers, known OCR angles, gold text, boundaries or expected counts. A gold-transcript diagnostic is an explicitly separate input condition, never a claim about end-to-end extraction. Its permitted input is literal physical text, observed reading order, coordinates and table-cell/layout geometry only. It must not include gold clinical header-to-value links, date/patient association scopes, encounter/report boundaries, semantic dossiers or correct dependency graphs. Any such relationships must be authored by the candidate from source evidence. Printed labels remain ordinary text; a geometric cell row/column is not an oracle clinical interpretation. Keep the semantic scoring oracle in a separate inaccessible file and audit the diagnostic input against this whitelist before dispatch.

Proposed initial scorer checks: **six semantic pairs, 12 valid representations**. (1) One document with six complete findings vs six records; (2) a one-page source locator vs an equivalent valid multi-page/region locator; (3) a shared note projected into Documents and Notes vs two linked views; (4) printed value plus an allowed explicit conversion vs the same equivalent representation; (5) a compound table assertion vs equivalent cell assertions with header links; (6) a proposal using an explicit referenced identity/date context vs directly attached equivalent evidence. Each pair must receive the same substantive score. Add a separately labeled mutation for each pair: absent finding, wrong page, duplicated accepted entity, disallowed conversion, swapped header, wrong person/date. Every mutation must fail its relevant endpoint. Add two provenance mutations retaining the correct value: one cites an undifferentiated whole 24-page document without identifying its supporting locations, and one cites valid but unrelated pages. Both must fail the source-support endpoint even though their IDs resolve and the value happens to match gold. Valid multi-page support remains accepted when it identifies and supports the relevant fields; require neither an arbitrary single page nor exact oracle bounding-box equality. These are 12 valid representations plus eight invalid mutations, **20 scored fixtures**, not provider jobs. Adapter rejection is reported separately from semantic failure; inspect disputes blind to candidate identity and append adjudication without silently rewriting original scores.

Add **six proposed offline identity-preflight cases**: native/local text with a mismatching explicitly patient-labeled DOB; a mismatching policyholder DOB beside matching patient evidence; matching name with no DOB; no identity; unfamiliar name with matching complete patient DOB; and incomplete/ambiguous patient DOB. Assert the specification’s distinct stop/pass/question states and known-name rule. A patient DOB mismatch detectable from native or locally available text must block broad clinical-AI reading of that file before dispatch, while other files proceed. “Read it anyway” authorizes reading, not Self attribution. For scans lacking adequate local identity evidence, declare the limited identity-reading boundary and uncertainty instead of claiming an early check was completed. A later patient-scope change still invalidates affected proposal eligibility.

Use a transport spy for these offline controls: local terminology operations must make no lookup-service request, and retain-only file bytes must never enter an AI payload.

Freeze code/config/source hashes, receipt schema, oracle version, comparable endpoint definitions, attempt IDs, proposed experimental time/token ceilings and review rules before any live launch. Define clinical-ready, text-retained, Done and Done-with-exceptions separately. Experimental ceilings control the study, never become product usage caps.

## Stage 1 — Falsify text-completeness claims independently of clinical reasoning

Proposed sample: **four development documents and eight reserved heldout documents**, each three pages, 36 total pages; each underlying template belongs to exactly one split. The eight heldout documents cover two independent examples of four families:

1. Native PDF with ordinary body text, repeated/administrative content, and a visible annotation or image text absent from the text layer.
2. Scan/photo with a small marginal note, rotated/faint text and legible handwriting beside a deliberately unreadable region.
3. Table/columns with a carried header, footnote changing a value's meaning, and a row continuing on the next page.
4. Re-export/correction with substantial repeated content but one changed number, negation or issue date; preserve both versions and both repeated locations.

Vary templates across split, not just image distortion. Human-readable glyph/layout truth should be independently checked against the actual rendered files. Use fictional handwriting from a reproducible fixture source; call it a handwriting simulation if it is synthetic. Do not claim a font proves real-handwriting generalization.

Compare a native-text-only negative control with the proposed complete-visual extraction path; add another engine only if the declared question is engine selection. Count every rendering, detection, native extraction, OCR/vision, retry and fallback operation. Run once per deterministic configuration; repeating the same pixels adds timing evidence, not document diversity. No clinical provider calls are needed for this stage unless a visual extractor itself uses a provider, which requires its own prior execution authorization/accounting.

**Primary endpoints:** (a) source spans falsely marked complete despite missing/changed readable content; (b) readable text correctly retained with original/page/region mapping; (c) readable spans unnecessarily declared uncertain; (d) truly unreadable regions correctly located and not guessed; (e) meaningful order/table-edge correctness. Report exact numerators/denominators by document and family, plus CER/WER only as diagnostics. OCR confidence, engine agreement and region boxes cannot supply the gold truth.

**Proposed finite-fixture gate:** zero false-complete readable spans, no invented words in unreadable regions, and all required visible content accounted for. A legible span replaced by an uncertainty exception avoids a false completeness claim but remains a failed readable-text extraction and a usability loss. Report it explicitly; an “everything uncertain” system cannot pass full-retention quality. Nominal clear-content controls should retain every adjudicated legible span and required relation. This strict fixture gate follows the full-text requirement; it is not a statistically proven universal guarantee or an arbitrary allowance for clinically relevant text loss. A failed extractor can be investigated while Stage 2 uses a gold transcript to test topology independently; it cannot receive an end-to-end pass.

For every arm, persist the complete extraction, restart, rebuild the derived search index from the durable package, then fetch every retained span by source location and query an administrative-only phrase with provider access disabled. Corrupt or omit one artifact in a separate negative control: recovery must report the missing evidence, never silently reconstruct via AI or claim intact text. Verify extraction correction history and authorization of unaccepted source text separately from accepted clinical records.

## Stage 2 — Small paired clinical topology screen

Use **two development documents** for adapter/prompts and freeze them, then **four new heldout documents**, all distinct from Stage 1 and old experiments. Proposed size is 24 pages each, with realistic mixed density: eight dense clinical/table pages, eight narrative/medication/history pages and eight administrative/repeated/mixed pages; report exact words, tables, image regions and gold fact counts before execution. Do not create a nominally long report almost entirely from filler. The small sample is for falsification, not 95% noninferiority claims.

Holdout categories:

- **H1: Remote qualifier.** Identity and report/specimen dates early; a clinically important table header/negation qualifier far from late values. Include a local plausible distractor and nonpatient DOB. The correct relationship is source-supported but crosses the bounded scope.
- **H2: Amendment and source-event identity.** Later pages correct an earlier result; upload order is reversed in a deterministic reconciliation replay. Include equal-value/date observations from different events, and an unchanged re-export source. Linking must preserve distinct assertions and issued-order versions.
- **H3: Subject/date scope change.** Self and relative/policyholder material occur in separate explicitly labeled sections; a later patient header contradicts a naive file-wide identity inheritance. No other-person clinical assertion may become Self. Unknown association must be explicit, without suppressing readable text.
- **H4: Long narrative with sparse actionable findings.** Meaningful narrative and administrative text with distributed positive, negated, historical and uncertain findings; medication prescription history plus an explicit stop suggestion, a partial printed date and one legible margin annotation. Retained text is complete even where no clinical proposal is appropriate.

First run H1/H2 as the same C0 composition with a forced B versus W reader interchange, **four jobs planned at most**, sequentially at concurrency one, paired on identical files and fixed order randomized before results. Begin with the **same adjudicated physical transcript/layout and original-region access** for both arms, following Stage 0’s explicit no-gold-semantic-relationships whitelist. This isolates context topology from OCR. Both must inspect every page or record an explicit uncompleted scope; retrieval of a few gold-like passages is not a full clinical pass. Keep all source evidence available, but never supply gold relationships. Freeze the scored safety boundary at the **completed composed clinical result after its declared assembly/validators**, or at an earlier unsafe escape: a wrong-person/unsupported critical claim becomes bulk-eligible, accepted, or presented as confirmed. A bad intermediate B or W draft that its designed safeguards reject before that boundary is recorded as a detected draft error, not an asymmetric reason to stop B before assembly. Log every draft error, correction, validation rejection and escaped error separately. A critical failure at the composed-result/escape boundary stops further spending on that frozen arm, preserving the result and all partial work for offline diagnosis. A noncritical gate failure or safe abstention remains a failed/incomplete quality endpoint but does not trigger that critical-stop rule; the unchanged arm continues its predeclared remaining fixtures unless an independent route/accounting/time stop applies.

If neither arm hits the critical-stop boundary, run H3/H4 in the same paired way, **four additional jobs**, preserving any earlier noncritical failures. If exactly one arm hits the critical stop, its remaining cells, including H2 if not yet dispatched and H3/H4, stay **unattempted**; the survivor may run those two fixtures (**two additional jobs, at most six total; fewer if the stopped arm never reached H2**) only when the pre-execution declaration says those unmeasured strata are useful for qualifying the remaining router path. Otherwise stop with that qualification unresolved. Survivor-only results do not establish a global winner, paired superiority or noninferiority. If both arms hit the critical stop, run no further clinical jobs; diagnose offline. This gives a maximum of eight initial heldout clinical jobs, not an architecture × unit-size × repeat matrix. Any repair requires a separately frozen amendment on development fixtures and a newly reserved heldout template for a positive repaired-candidate claim; do not optimize against these four scores.

**Primary clinical endpoints:** exact source-supported atomic fact recall and precision; complete relation recall for subject/date/specimen/negation/unit/header/amendment; critical incorrect assertions; unsupported assertions; genuinely distinct source events collapsed; duplicate machine assertions; valid, resolvable supporting locations; and all-page clinical-scope coverage. A fact is correct only with its required qualifiers/links. Enumerate required product fields/actions per kind before execution, so output shape alone neither fails nor rescues an arm. Clinical abstention stays in the recall denominator and is reported alongside false assertions; “all uncertain” is not a clinical pass.

**Proposed finite-fixture gate:** zero wrong-person or unsupported critical value/negation/date-association claims, zero silent omitted pages or destructive merges, all expected assertions/relations correctly represented, and complete measured coverage. An explicit unresolved review item is a safe abstention when genuinely uncertain, but remains a missing correct assertion/relation and prevents a full clinical-quality pass for that fixture. Report it as incomplete quality, not success. This screen does not establish the original broad F2 quality margin or universal safety. For an eligible composition or routing preference within the declared fixture stratum, require comparable finished workload and no meaningful quality/review deficit on any fixture. If one completes and another fails, report capability/quality evidence; do not call the partial attempt a faster result. If both pass with similar cost, retain both eligible component options and prefer the simpler maintained composition for that declared source stratum pending broader evidence. Do not force one route onto every document.

## Stage 3 — Scheduling/recovery and review races, fake transport

Use one fictional six-page composite with text artifacts, two identity scopes, an early proposal and late amendment, plus a second-profile sentinel. No real provider is needed. Test the shared durable pipeline once where implementations are actually identical; test each candidate's distinct task/checkpoint boundary separately. Proposed **ten fault/transition cases**, with uninterrupted control state and expected receipts frozen:

1. Interrupted upload before atomic original commit.
2. Worker failure after output artifact commit but before scheduler acknowledgement.
3. Provider response lost after remote completion; retry duplicates remote work but never local proposal/approval.
4. Crash between text persistence and index publication; rebuild retains all text without AI.
5. Crash between clinical proposal persistence and completion checkpoint.
6. Browser close, sign-out and profile switch while an explicitly authorized background capability runs; old interactive authorization is revoked and the other profile cannot inspect it.
7. Process restart loses in-memory capability; no private work before unlock, automatic resume after authorized unlock.
8. Provider-limit wait followed by success; no terminal “failed” status, no new user action, wait-aware estimate and all attempts counted.
9. Stop imports during an in-flight call, then restart; no new dispatch after stop, in-flight work has an explicit preserved disposition and no duplicated publication.
10. Early review then late amendment; stale bulk selection is revalidated, accepted history is not silently changed, and a durable receipt identifies the approved revision.

For each, compare final originals, text revisions, proposals, accepted versions, source links, unresolved exceptions and approval receipts to the declared control semantics. Exceptions must appear once. Any cross-profile access, acceptance without approval, lost durable evidence or duplicated accepted version is a hard failure. Background tests require a reviewed key/capability contract; do not bypass current locking code or pretend the current session contract already permits them. If only a prototype capability model exists, label the result as model verification, not application acceptance.

## Stage 4 — Minimal end-to-end and length checks, only after the screens

First test C0 with its normal router and one eligible component interchange on **two newly reserved six-page documents**, one annotation/table and one scanned narrative/identity challenge: **four jobs maximum** for this initial integration screen. Choose the single interchange before gold is opened, based on the component uncertainty that remains; do not multiply every extractor by every reader. Log the route predicate and exact component path for each job. Before opening heldout results, reserve at least one of these fixtures to exercise the declared alternate/fallback path through an observable trigger: for example, visible annotation text missing from the native layer that requires the proposed visual extractor, or an unsupported input-fit/dependency condition that selects the eligible alternate clinical route. The trigger and policy must be fixed in advance and detected from runtime evidence, not gold answers or a manually selected winning result. Qualify a claimed fallback only if the real composed job actually traverses that branch and its resulting retained text/clinical output passes the same endpoints, with all earlier attempts counted. If the branch never activates, or if only fake receipts activate it, mark its live quality/compatibility qualification **unmet**; the six fake routing cases do not supply that evidence. The initial four-job budget need not test every imaginable fallback: only the observed, predeclared branch can gain scoped qualification. Other branches remain unqualified until a separately authorized reserved-fixture test. Compare these with predeclared gold-transcript controls only as paired diagnostics; every real extraction cost belongs in the end-to-end total. A failure here distinguishes a transcript/visual evidence defect from a topology defect and blocks adoption despite previous gold-input passes.

If a plausible topology or routing difference remains worth measuring, use **one independent realistic document family at 12 and 48 pages** with the same predeclared density mix as Stage 2 and a fixed per-page information distribution. Include remote dependencies at proportional distances, plus constant document-level identity/header content. Gold fact counts grow with length; publish density, expected output size and fixed-versus-growing components. These are related fixtures, not independent clinical cohorts. Run the baseline and one eligible topology interchange once at both lengths: **four jobs**, at concurrency one, with route selections and boundaries predeclared. Before either length runs, freeze the deliberately simple proportional null hypothesis for every stage resource metric: **predicted M48 = 4 × measured M12**, separately for input tokens, output tokens, total tokens, requests, and active stage duration. Apply the same formula to the completed-job totals; wall time is a separate conditional 4× scenario, never a reliable forecast of provider waits. Do not fit a power exponent from one observation. **Fixed setup is not subtracted or estimated:** it remains in M12 and is therefore multiplied by four; record independently identifiable setup work separately to explain this known bias, without retroactively changing the forecast. Any alternative fixed-plus-variable analytic model requires its exact formula and externally available parameters to be declared before the 12-page outcome, not guessed afterward. Freeze the numeric proportional predictions after the complete 12-page receipt and before dispatching/exposing the 48-page result. A failed or incompletely accounted short arm yields NOT_ESTIMABLE, not a fabricated successful-completion extrapolation. A zero baseline for a metric predicts zero; report absolute residual there and do not divide by zero.

Report actual/predicted residuals separately for extraction, clinical reading, assembly/audit and total. Do not assume output is constant when facts grow, or infer tokens from characters. Proposed screening tolerance for input/output/total tokens and requests is ±25% of the frozen prediction, with any time residual exploratory only; this is a model diagnostic, not a clinical gate or confidence interval. If the prediction fails, the result is “this extrapolation failed,” not permission to refit and relabel it validated. Four-times pages is only a local scaling probe, not evidence beyond 48 pages.

Only after topology and recovery are characterized, test concurrency separately: the selected frozen composition and router on **four independent identical-policy job copies**, once with concurrency one and once with concurrency two, same total workload and gold scoring. These are eight job executions and a throughput experiment, not more clinical diversity. Record per-job latency, makespan, waits, CPU/memory, contention, request retries and invariants. Skip this optional stage unless speed remains the actual bottleneck and a further live budget is explicitly authorized. It must not be smuggled into the initial eight-job screen.

## How earlier evidence constrains this new plan

The original experiments and their frozen scores remain unchanged. They are regression evidence, not a new holdout and not proof that these new components are implemented:

- The scripted unit-size study showed predictable request-count reductions and successful N400 interpolation, while live dense costs/times did not improve monotonically with bigger units. Fix scope policy for the topology test; do not repeat a broad unit-size sweep or use scripted savings as a live latency forecast.
- Mixed-document live failures implicated lost early context and strict provenance/association handling. Challenge source relationships directly, and validate the new representation adapter before attributing a format rejection to failed clinical reasoning. Neither a valid locator nor retained text alone proves the relationship is correct.
- The long-report supplemental attempts did not establish complete clinical extraction or a valid live length extrapolation. A partial document or observation is still incomplete against its frozen original endpoint; its existence motivates neutral assertion scoring in the new experiment, not rewriting old results as passes. Separately retained administrative text is now required and must be tested directly.
- OCR, fingerprint/layout and identity extensions found different failure mechanisms. OCR quality/latency gates did not all pass; fingerprint retrieval success did not imply exact unchanged content or correct encounter segmentation. Keep visual coverage, text retention, candidate matching, unchanged-text verification and patient-role association separate.
- Authentication interruptions and unknown usage exposed an accounting problem, not a text-quality result. Preserve those recordings; new experiments require their own reviewed route/attempt accounting and cannot call a rejected request zero-cost without evidence.

These findings justify targeted controls and component boundaries. They do not force every source through the same engine or clinical reader.

## All-stage accounting and execution controls

For every stage and every attempt retain immutable source/config/oracle hashes, software versions, raw request/response or offline operation receipts, wall start/end, active time, provider waiting, durable commits, result status and independent verification. Track rendering, native extraction, OCR/vision, text persistence/indexing, clinical inference, retrieval, assembly/audit, validation and retries separately. Input, cached-input and output/reasoning tokens follow actual provider receipts; cached input is a subset, not extra total tokens. Unknown usage remains unknown, with a known subtotal. A reconstruction is an estimate in a separate column, never a fabricated charge receipt. Include setup probes and failed requests in cost.

Measure time to **first valid reviewable proposal**, full-text readiness, all clinical work terminal, complete-job duration and user questions; do not equate any first JSON with useful output. Report end-to-end and stage costs, maximum payload, peak memory/storage and duplication of source transmission. Show complete successes separately from failures/censoring; no averages hiding unattempted cells. Dollar costs depend on the actual route and should not be invented for a subscription route.

Before any live execution, the owner/coordinator must approve the concrete finite plan, model/route and cumulative experimental limits under the repository rules; this document authorizes none. Use the already reviewed single-run interruption protocol where applicable: preserve all partials, stop future dispatch on unknown accounting/auth failure, and resume only with independently reviewed same-run state and cumulative allowances. Do not reset clocks/token totals or drop failed costs. Each experiment's dispatch deadline may drain its in-flight request, with the grace cost/time reported. Provider waits are a product waiting state but can censor a time-bounded experiment; record that distinction honestly.

An independent verifier must rederive stage counts, source/fact/relationship scores, durability checks and cost totals from the recorded inputs; the designer/runner cannot award their own pass. Deliver a proposed-decision record with each claim supported, falsified or unresolved, not one global “positive” score masking missing arms. A small screen can reject a bad prototype; it cannot certify arbitrary medical documents.

## Scope and stop sequence

The smallest initially executable comparison is Stage 0 plus the component/adapter contracts and six fake routing boundary cases, then H1/H2 × B/W inside the same durable composition: up to four clinical jobs with fixed physical gold evidence. No OCR topology cross-product, four unit sizes, five repeats or concurrency sweep is needed to expose a cross-page failure. The next four heldout jobs occur only if useful. Engine/retention and recovery tests run independently so failures are assigned to the responsible stage. End-to-end, scaling and throughput work are conditional extensions, each separately costed and authorized; not a promise to run every proposed job automatically.

All three finalized reports were read only after their independent drafting ended. This document is a proposed executable specification. No OCR, provider, import, or fault-injection experiments were executed to create it, and none are authorized by publication.
